# Self-Billing Agreement — living decision doc

**Topic:** how the platform records that a cleaner **agreed in advance** to have invoices issued in their
name and on their behalf (*samofakturace* / self-billing) — what is recorded, who owns the text, when it
binds, and what happens to a cleaner nobody has asked yet.

## The problem this area exists to solve

The platform issues the cleaner's invoice **for** the cleaner: on the document the cleaner is the
supplier (*Dodavatel*) and Cleansia the customer (*Odběratel*). That is self-billing, and self-billing
rests on the supplier having agreed to it **in advance**. T-0522 shipped the document. Nothing shipped
the agreement.

So today the platform generates documents whose legal basis is a contract clause the owner is *adding*,
with **no per-cleaner record that anyone agreed to anything**. `rg -i 'samofaktur|self.?bill'` over
`src/` returns zero hits — no column, no enum value, no string, no PDF marking.

## The current shape (ADR-0041, proposed)

```
AgreementVersion          (Kind, CountryId, Version, EffectiveFrom)          -- the terms, over time
  └── AgreementVersionText (LanguageCode, Body, ReviewStatus)                -- one row per language

EmployeeAgreementAcceptance   -- APPEND-ONLY. Never updated, never deleted.
  EmployeeId · Kind · Action(Accepted|Revoked) · AgreementVersionId
  LanguageCode · BodyHash(sha256, server-computed) · OccurredAt
  Channel(PartnerWeb|PartnerMobile|AdminRecordedContract) · IpAddress · DeviceLabel
  RecordedByUserId? · ContractReference?

EmployeeInvoice.SelfBillingAcceptanceId  -- nullable FK, stamped at issuance, frozen
```

Current state = **the latest row for `(employee, kind)`**, never "a row exists".

### The five rules that carry the design

1. **It is not a `ConsentType`.** A commercial contract term filed into the GDPR consent register would
   be withdrawable by its subject through a live GDPR endpoint with a one-field POST — and the existing
   table has no version and overwrites its own evidence on re-grant.
2. **The version is not optional, and the server stamps it.** The client *echoes* the version and the
   language it displayed; a mismatch is **rejected** (`agreement.version_stale`), never silently upgraded.
   An un-updated app must not be able to record agreement to text it did not show.
3. **The text is server-owned and provenance-gated.** No `BusinessSupplied`+ text for a country ⇒
   **no checkbox, no gate, nothing rendered.** The platform never demands agreement to words nobody
   wrote. (This is the shipped `LegalNoticeReviewStatus` rule applied to a second surface.)
4. **"Mandatory" binds at onboarding submit, and nowhere else.** Not `IsProfileComplete()`, not the job
   board, not `GenerateInvoice`. Every cleaner alive today fails this term; a completeness term would
   403 the entire partner surface on deploy day — the outage ADR-0034 §D7 exists to prevent.
5. **The un-asked cohort is made visible, not blocked.** Every payout invoice is stamped with the
   acceptance that authorized it, so *"which documents were issued without an agreement?"* is a `WHERE
   … IS NULL` query, and the admin report splits never-accepted / stale-version / contract-only.

## The trade-off space

| Option | For | Against | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1 — `ConsentType.SelfBilling`** | Reuses everything; one enum value | Withdrawable through the GDPR endpoint (no allow-list); no version; `Regrant` destroys prior evidence; files a payment term into the lawful-basis register | **Rejected.** Grant *mechanics* copied (server-read IP/device, no-op on already-granted) |
| **A2 — `Employee.SelfBillingAcceptedAt`** | One additive column; always materialized | Cannot say *which text*; cannot express re-consent; cannot distinguish a tick from a signature; sitting on `Employee` it invites a `IsProfileComplete()` term = platform-wide 403 | **Rejected** — it is the stale boolean, written down |
| **A3 — server owns the version, clients own the text** | **One table, not three**; reuses the shipped `ConsentMarkup`/`ConsentMarkdown` + 5-locale `ConsentCatalog` guards; no legal-text API | An installed app renders **old bytes under the current version key**, undetectably — and two clients are app-store gated. Every wording change = 7 catalogs × 5 locales + two store releases | **Rejected — but it is the strongest alternative and the panel should re-test it.** See ADR-0041 §CH-A1 |
| **A4 — snapshot the full text per acceptance** | Unarguable years later | Duplicates a paragraph per cleaner per version; invites a second display source that can disagree | **Rejected** — `BodyHash` buys the tamper-evidence for 64 chars |
| **A5 — block invoice issuance (the ADR-0034 D7 shape)** | Symmetrical with the payout gate | ADR-0034 blocks an *unusable* document (no payee account); here the document is usable and only our corroboration is missing. Withholds a tax document for up to ~31 days (**monthly** periods), re-creates D7.2's fiscal-reconciliation noise, and hits **100%** of the population on day one | **Rejected** — D6's stamp gets the visibility with none of the harm |
| **A6 — contract clause only, nothing in software** | The contract *is* the legal basis | The pre-clause cohort is covered by neither instrument, and with no record we cannot know who they are or when that stops being true | **Rejected** — the checkbox's value over the contract is per-cleaner, per-version, timestamped evidence |

## Traps this area has already stepped on once

- **A gate that trips on everyone.** ADR-0034 §F2/§D7 caught a completeness term that would have 403'd
  every cleaner off the whole partner surface. This design adds a term **100%** of the population fails.
  Rule 4 is not a preference; it is the same defect refusing to recur.
- **A tenant-less sweep reading a tenant-scoped row.** The invoice stamp is resolved from
  `PayPeriodBackgroundService`, which has **no tenant claim**. A scoped read there resolves
  `TenantId == null` against tenanted rows and reports the safe-sounding *"no agreement"* — under any
  blocking design that withholds **every invoice in every tenant** (security-rules **S8**, third form).
  Both repositories carry the `…IgnoringTenantAsync` sibling and the pinning test seeds a **non-null**
  `TenantId`.
- **A navigation-walking erasure.** `GdprDeletionService` loads `Employee → Address` and nothing else,
  and there is **no lazy loading** — so a clear reaching through a navigation is a silent no-op that
  returns success (ADR-0034 D8.8's worked example). Redaction is an **id-keyed** repository call.
- **`ContractStatus` is a false friend.** It is the admin approval state wearing a legal name; hanging
  the agreement off it would let a back-office click manufacture legal consent, and it would inherit the
  confirmed `Deactivated()` divergence (an erased cleaner still reads `Approved`).
- **A fixture that supplies an input production never produces.** T-0522 hit it twice on this exact
  document. The erasure test and the tenancy test here both fail that way by default — hand-populated
  navigations and null-tenant fixtures pass green while production is broken.

## Where the seams are

- **Per country** — one `AgreementVersion` row. Adding SK, DE or PL costs **zero migrations and zero
  code**; no handler branches on a country code (ADR-0017). The jurisdiction is
  `WorkCountryId ?? Address.CountryId`, reusing ADR-0034 §D2 verbatim.
- **Per agreement kind** — one enum value. The signed B2B contract, a DPA, a code of conduct are the
  same shape. *(Note the deliberate contrast with ADR-0034 D1: there, cardinality was pinned at one;
  here many rows per (employee, kind) **is the feature**, because that is what re-consent means.)*
- **Per audience** — the self-service endpoints live on the **partner web** and **partner mobile** hosts
  only; the admin record-on-behalf command on the admin host. Customer hosts are untouched. No host
  learns about another.
- **Per client** — only the **body** comes from the server; heading, checkbox label, "read full text"
  and error strings stay in the client i18n catalogs where the existing `ConsentCatalog` guards live.
