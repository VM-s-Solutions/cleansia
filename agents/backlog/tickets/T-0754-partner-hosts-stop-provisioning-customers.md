## Context

Owner ruling 2026-09-15: *"Remove it."* Both partner `AuthController`s routed the customer
`Register.Command` on an anonymous `POST Register`, and both routed `GoogleAuth`, which on a first
sign-in **provisioned a Customer** (`User.CreateWithGoogle` leaves the default `UserProfile.Customer`)
and minted a partner-audience token for it — an account that could sign in where it had no business.
No shipped client called either: the partner web's `PartnerAuthService.register()` and
`authenticateWithGoogle` had spec callers only; the Android and iOS partner apps had zero references.
The `GoogleAuth` branch was named as a deliberate addition: it is the same defect one step further.

## Doing

- `Register` deleted from both partner `AuthController`s (`Web.Partner`, `Web.Mobile.Partner`).
- On a host that is not a customer host, `GoogleAuth` signs in an existing Employee or Administrator
  **only**: a first-time identity is refused `auth.social_account_not_found` and nothing is
  provisioned; a Customer-profile account is refused `auth.insufficient_privileges` — `PartnerLogin`'s
  rule, decided by `IHostAudienceProvider` inside the handler, not by a second command. HostTests on
  both partner hosts pin it (a new identity → 4xx with the key, no user row; a Customer account →
  refused).
- Review (`362f97ef`): `TokenService.GenerateTokenAsync` refuses an audience/profile pair the host
  does not serve as an **invariant** (`InvalidOperationException` — the command is expected to have
  refused with its own key first), and `ConfirmUserEmail` off a customer host confirms an Employee or
  Administrator only, on the same rule.
- `SessionAuditRouteTests` rewritten for the missing route, `AuditGateTests` docstring, the partner
  parity spec's comment and its hard-coded `'AuthController -> Auth/Register.cs ->
  OperatorTenantScopeBehavior'` scoped line removed, HostTests route rosters updated.
- Clients and the partner mobile spec regenerated by the orchestrator (`1f3c70b8`) — a deliberate
  contract shrink; the partner web's dead `register()` dropped with it.

## NOT

No change to the customer hosts' `Register` / `GoogleAuth` / `AppleAuth`. No change to
`RegisterEmployee`. Android `AuthInterceptor.ANON_ENDPOINTS` (a `contains` match that also covers
`registeremployee`) stays.
