# ADR-0058 — A customer's market is chosen, remembered, and overridden by the address

- **Status:** `accepted` — **2026-09-13**, after the architect panel's challenge round and the
  programme's six implementation lanes (T-0711, T-0712, T-0714, T-0716, T-0718 and their copy
  siblings). Where the tree departs from the draft, §Consequences says so.
- **Date:** 2026-09-13
- **Supersedes:** — (no ADR stated the earlier rule; two **docs** sentences did and are revised, see
  §Consequences)
- **Superseded by:** —
- **Builds on:** the multicurrency programme as shipped (`Currency.IsActive` as the market switch,
  prices authored per currency, `ICurrencyResolutionService.ResolveCurrencyForCountryAsync`,
  `GET …/GetOverview?countryId=` on the catalogue) and ADR-0056's precedent that per-country
  presentation data hangs off `Country` / `CountryConfiguration`. Siblings: **ADR-0059** (Plus priced
  per market) and **ADR-0060** (copy figures per market) both *read* the market this ADR defines.
- **Applies to:** `Cleansia.Core.AppServices/Features/Markets`, `Cleansia.Web.Customer`,
  `Cleansia.Web.Mobile.Customer`, `Cleansia.App` (customer app, SSR), `cleansia_android/customer-app`,
  `cleansia_ios/CleansiaCustomer`
- **Owner rulings this ADR implements (2026-09-12):** R-market — *"Customer-chosen market, defaulting
  to CZ"*: a market selector on the customer surfaces, remembered like the language, defaulting to the
  platform default; prices, copy and the Plus subscription follow it; a booking's address still
  overrides it; works for anonymous visitors; expansion = switch a market on. R3 — the home
  quick-quote market chip: *"Yup, I'd like to have the one."*

> **One decision:** what a customer's *market* is, where the choice lives, what wins when two signals
> disagree, and which surfaces read it. It is one decision because the four parts are one contract:
> a client that persists the choice in the wrong place renders the wrong market on the server; a
> precedence rule without a named reader list becomes "whatever each screen does"; and the expansion
> story is only true if every reader resolves through the same seam.

## Context

### What the tree did before this programme (branch `fix/remove-membership-free-trial`, verified by reading)

| Fact | Where |
|---|---|
| The **order** currency is the service address's country's currency; the quote takes `CountryId` (address) and falls to the platform default with none. Nothing about the customer decides it. | `QuoteOrder.ResolveQuoteCurrencyId`, `CreateOrder`, `ICurrencyResolutionService` |
| Catalogue overviews take `?countryId=` and answer in ONE currency, labelled (`CurrencyCode` on every list DTO). | `GetServiceOverview`, `ServiceController` and twins |
| **Every pre-address customer surface passed no country** and therefore rendered the platform default: home, quick quote, `/services`, `/plus`, `/rewards`, wizard step 0, both mobile home tabs, both membership screens. | the programme's plumbing scout |
| The language choice was the model for "remembered like the language": web = localStorage **and a cookie** so SSR renders it; Android = DataStore `app_settings`; iOS = `UserDefaults` via `AppSettingsStore`. | `translation-loader.service.ts`, `AppSettingsRepository.kt`, `AppSettingsStore.swift` |
| The customer SSR render can see **only the request** (cookies + headers). A preference held only in localStorage renders the default on the server and repaints on hydrate — the flash the language cookie exists to prevent. | `app.routes.server.ts`, `translation-loader.service.ts`, `/architecture/frontend` |
| The customer hosts already exposed anonymous `Country/GetServiced` and `Currency/GetOverview`; neither said which currency a country uses. `CountryConfiguration.DefaultCurrencyCode` was the only link and no customer endpoint exposed it. | `CountryController`, `CurrencyController`, `CountryListItem`, `CountryConfiguration` |
| Docs stated the pre-R-market rule: *"The market is a property of the booking, not of the customer … no currency picker on any customer surface, and there will not be one."* | `platform-expandability.md`, `business-rules.md` — both revised by this programme |

### What the owner's ruling changes, and what it does not

R-market adds a **second market signal on the customer side**: a chosen one, for the surfaces that
have no address yet. It does **not** change the order rule — the address still decides what an order
is priced and charged in (`CreateOrder` refuses a mismatching explicit currency and re-prices). So the
chosen market is a *browsing* context, and the address is the *booking* context. The docs sentence
"the market is a property of the booking" stays true of the order; it stops being true of the
catalogue, the Plus page and the copy, which now have a market before there is a booking.

## Decision

### D1 — A market is a serviced country with a configured currency. There is no new entity.

A **market** is a `Country` row that satisfies all of:

1. `IsServiced && IsActive` (the Service Area page's switch — `PUT api/AdminCountry/{id}/serviced`);
2. it has a `CountryConfiguration` row whose `DefaultCurrencyCode` names an existing `Currency`
   (the same lookup `ResolveCurrencyForCountryAsync` performs, without the throw — a serviced country
   that fails it is **not listed**, and is logged as a configuration defect);
3. that currency `IsActive` (the market switch).

The market's **identity** on the wire is the `Country` row (`CountryId` for calls, `IsoCode` for
persistence — see D3). Its **currency** is the configured one. Two countries may share a currency (SK
and DE both EUR); a market is the *country*, not the currency, because copy, presets (ADR-0056), legal
text and the insurance figure (ADR-0060) vary by country while sharing a unit.

**Exposed by one anonymous read on both customer hosts** — `GET api/Market/GetOverview` →
`IReadOnlyList<MarketListItem>`: `countryId`, `isoCode` (alpha-3, what a client persists),
`isoAlpha2` (what the chip prints), `name` + `translations`, `currencyId` / `currencyCode` /
`currencySymbol`, `isDefault`, and the two per-market copy figures ADR-0060 defines (`noShowCredit`,
`insuranceCoverageAmount`). One call gives a client everything a pre-address surface needs; before it
that took two calls (`Country/GetServiced` + `Currency/GetOverview`) and still could not join them.
The handler is `GetMarkets` (`IRequest`, no validator, no `BusinessResult`), ordered as the serviced
countries come.

**The one column this decision adds is `Country.IsoAlpha2`** (required, two upper-case letters,
seeded for all 249 ISO 3166-1 rows, required on `CreateCountry`, optional-if-sent on `UpdateCountry`,
`country.iso_alpha2_invalid` otherwise). The draft carried no alpha-2 and the design pass asked the
question (Q-DESIGN-01: print `CZE · CZK`, add a column, or keep a per-client map). The column was
chosen: the chip reads "CZ · CZK" as the owner wrote it, and three per-client maps that can drift are
what the market directory exists to remove. `IsoCode` stays alpha-3 because clients persist it and the
seed references it; nothing keys on the alpha-2.

- **Rejected — extend `Country/GetServiced` / `CountryListItem` with currency fields.** `CountryListItem`
  is shared with the admin overview; and "serviced" (where you may *book*) is a weaker predicate than
  "market" (where you may *browse priced*): a serviced country whose configuration is missing throws in
  the resolver. The stricter predicate needs its own read. (`CountryListItem` did gain `isoAlpha2`,
  because the partner app reads it for its service-area map — see §Consequences.)
- **Rejected — extend `CurrencyListItem`.** A currency is not a market (SK and DE share EUR).
- **Rejected — a `Market` table or `Country.IsMarket` flag.** Nothing to store that the three rows do
  not already hold; a second writer would only let them disagree.

### D2 — The default market is the market whose currency is the platform default currency

`Currency.IsDefault` already defines what a no-country request is priced in
(`ResolveCurrencyForCountryAsync(null)`). For "default market" to mean *what you get before choosing*,
its currency must be that one; so **the default market is the market whose configured currency is the
default currency**. Today: CZE/CZK.

**At most one row carries `IsDefault`, and the read never throws.** Two default-currency markets is
**not** a misconfiguration — it is the ordinary state the day EUR is the default and SVK and DEU are
both serviced — and `Market/GetOverview` is the anonymous read behind the landing page, so a throw
there is a 500 on `/` for every visitor. The rule, as `GetMarkets.ChooseDefaultAsync` implements it:

- **one** market on the default currency → it is `IsDefault`;
- **several** → the one with the lowest `IsoCode` (ordinal, reseed-proof) is `IsDefault`, and the
  handler `LogError`s naming the currency and every candidate — the owner is expected to see that log
  and pick explicitly (below);
- **none** (the default currency's country is not serviced, or no currency is default) → no row is
  `IsDefault`; the handler `LogError`s; clients pre-select the **first listed** market (D3). Nothing
  breaks: a client always sends the `countryId` it resolved (D4), so pricing never depends on
  `IsDefault` being set.

The "default market" is a **pre-selection**, not a pricing invariant. The convenience that "default
market = no country" (D4) holds in the one-market case and is not relied on anywhere.

**Explicit choice deferred, not rejected:** a `CountryConfiguration.IsDefaultMarket` flag (with the
admin writer `CountryConfiguration` does not have) is the right shape once two markets share the
default currency. It is not built now because the case does not exist and the flag would be a second
source that can disagree with `Currency.IsDefault` until an admin form exists to keep them aligned.
The ISO-code tiebreak plus the error log is the placeholder; the owner question is Q-MARKET-01.

`SetDefaultCurrency` therefore also **moves the default market** — added to the standing list of
things that promotion re-denominates (`/product/business-rules#money-constants`).

- **Rejected — the active `CompanyInfo.CountryId`** (the ADR-0035 AM-10 "platform default country"
  chain). It is a tenant-scoped read on an anonymous endpoint, and a second source that can disagree
  with the default currency: a company registered in CZ with the default currency promoted to EUR
  would produce a default market whose catalogue is not the default catalogue.

### D3 — Where the choice lives: a device-local preference, keyed by ISO code; on the web the cookie is the only store

| Client | Store | Key | Value | Why this store |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Web (customer app) | a cookie `path=/; max-age=31536000; SameSite=Lax` — **and nothing else** (`market-preference.ts`) | `preferred_market` | ISO-3166 alpha-3 as `Country.IsoCode` (`CZE`) | the cookie is the only per-visitor input the SSR render can read, and `document.cookie` is readable in the browser, so one store serves both branches |
| Android | DataStore `app_settings` (`AppSettingsRepository`) | `market` | ISO code, absent = default | beside `language` and `theme` |
| iOS | `UserDefaults` via `UserDefaultsAppSettingsStore`, behind a `MarketPreferenceStore` protocol in `CleansiaCore` | `settings.market` | ISO code, nil = default | beside `settings.language`; a separate protocol rather than three new requirements on `AppSettingsStore`, so the partner app's five fake conformers keep compiling |

**Why not the language's localStorage + cookie pair.** Two stores can disagree: the cookie expires
at a year or is cleared by the browser while localStorage is kept, and the language's browser branch
reads **localStorage**, never the cookie. For the market that disagreement is a **hydration mismatch**:
the server renders CZE from no cookie, the client resolves SVK from localStorage, every catalogue URL
differs (`?countryId=`), and the page repaints — the flash the cookie was added to prevent, produced by
the second store. One store, read by both branches, cannot disagree with itself. (The language pair
carries the same latent defect; it is a reported finding, not absorbed here.)

**ISO code, not `Country.Id`.** Ids are ULIDs minted by the seed; the DEV drop (owed at every migration
regen) re-mints them, and a cookie or a DataStore holding a stale id would silently fall to the default
on every environment rebuild. The code is stable across reseeds and readable in a request log. The
client maps code → id through `Market/GetOverview` at bootstrap.

**Resolution at bootstrap, every client, in this order:** stored code **if** it names a listed market
→ else the `IsDefault` market → else the first listed market → persist the result (browser and mobile
only; the server branch never writes). A stored code naming a market that has since been switched
off falls to the default and is overwritten, not kept. **A stored value is only ever compared against
the list, never rendered or sent:** a cookie is attacker-controlled text; the client emits the
matched `MarketListItem`'s own `IsoCode`/`CountryId`, and an unmatched value is dropped. There is no
Accept-Language, region or IP inference: a locale is not a market, and any non-cookie signal would
need its own SSR cache key.

**When `Market/GetOverview` fails at bootstrap** (API down, network) — and, on both mobile apps, when
it answers an **empty** list: the stored code cannot be mapped to an id, so every D5 reader sends
**no `countryId`** (server semantics: the default currency), labels come from each payload's own
`CurrencyCode` as they do today, **the chip and the selector are not rendered** (there is no market to
name — nothing renders a guessed unit), nothing is persisted, and the list is retried — on the web on
the next `NavigationEnd` while the store is `loadFailed`, on Android on the next home refresh
(`onResume`, pull-to-refresh), on iOS through a 30-second staleness watermark that treats the
unavailable state as always stale. Both mobile repositories keep the **last** successfully loaded list
across a failed refresh. On the web the server-side fetch is a plain `HttpClient` GET; the SSR render
does **not** fail on this call — it renders the no-market shape.

**Not synced to the server.** `User.PreferredLanguageCode` is synced because e-mails and pushes are
rendered server-side in it; nothing server-side renders in a *market* — every order carries its own
currency and every push/e-mail formats from the order. A `User.PreferredMarketCode` column would be a
second writer with a sync protocol and no reader.
- **Rejected — server-side `User.PreferredMarketCode`.** No server reader; anonymous visitors are the
  main case; a signed-in customer switching devices re-picks (defaults to the default market), which
  is the language's behaviour too.
- **Rejected — localStorage only on the web** (the theme's store). Renders the default market on the
  server for a visitor who chose another, then repaints.
- **Rejected — localStorage + cookie** (the language's pair). See above.

**SSR consequences, stated:** the landing micro-cache serves only cookie-less requests, so a visitor
who has chosen a market bypasses it (as one with a language cookie already does) and a cookie-less
visitor is served the default market — correct by D3's resolution. **The hydration invariant:** for a
given request, the server branch (cookie header) and the browser branch (`document.cookie`) read the
same value against the same list, so they resolve the same market and issue the same URLs — the
client's first render equals the server's. Whether the browser *re-fetches* those URLs is a separate
matter, and today it does — see §Consequences.

**Anonymous is a web-only case.** Both mobile apps gate every screen behind sign-in, so on mobile the
preference is always a signed-in customer's, and Profile → Preferences is reachable. On the web the
selector is in the navbar/footer of every public route and needs no session.

### D4 — Precedence: address > chosen market > default

For any read that has a currency, the country it is resolved from is, in order:

1. **the service address's country** — when the surface has one (wizard from the address step on,
   recurring from the saved address, an existing order's own stamped currency);
2. **the chosen market** — every surface before there is an address;
3. **the default market** — when nothing is chosen (D3 makes this the persisted value, so in practice
   a client always sends a `countryId`).

Concretely: a client **always sends `countryId`** (the address's or the market's) to
`Service|Package|Extra/GetOverview`, `QuoteOrder`, `QuotePlusSavings`, `Membership/GetPlans` (ADR-0059),
`CreateMembershipCheckoutSession` / `CreateMembershipSubscription` (ADR-0059). Server semantics do not
change: a named country resolves to its configured currency or throws; null is the default. Because
D2 makes the default market's currency the default currency, "default market" and "no country" are
the same answer, so no server code path is added for the default case.

**The address wins silently; the market never overrides an address.** When the wizard reaches the
address step, `followAddressCountry()` reloads and trims the basket for the address country and shows
`catalogue_changed_for_country` — that sentence is market-agnostic and stays. A market chosen *after*
an address is entered does not touch that booking. On Android and iOS the booking view model combines
the two sources the same way (`address ?? market`), and closing the sheet hands the home tab back to
the market.

**The one thing inside a booking that does NOT follow the address: the Plus offer.** The wizard's
Plus step lists plans and opens checkout in the **chosen market's** currency (rule 2), even when the
booking around it is priced in the address's (rule 1). A subscription belongs to the customer, not to
the booking (ADR-0059 D2: it keeps its currency for life), and the owner's R-market puts "the Plus
subscription" on the market side of the line. The step's *savings* figure (`QuotePlusSavings`) is the
basket's currency, as today — Plus's benefits are a percentage and waivers, so a EUR subscription
saves CZK on a CZK booking without conversion (ADR-0059 D2). The step labels the two figures with
their own codes; when they differ that is honest, not a defect. When the market lists no plan the web
wizard **skips** the step on Next/Back and, if opened from the stepper, renders the "not available"
block above the decline row.

**The insurance figure inside a booking follows rule 1.** The mobile trust badge on the confirm step
formats the **address country's** market figure when that country is listed, the chosen market's
before there is an address, and the no-figure variant otherwise (ADR-0060 D2).

### D5 — What reads the chosen market, and what does not

**Reads it (customer surfaces, all three clients):**

| Surface | What changes |
|---|---|
| Home catalogue strips, `/services` | `GetOverview?countryId=<market>` instead of none; labels from the payload's `CurrencyCode` |
| Home quick quote + the R3 **market chip** | `QuoteOrderCommand.countryId = <market>`; the chip prints `{isoAlpha2} · {currencyCode}` and opens the selector; the property-size presets come from `Country/GetPropertySizes?isoCode=<market>` (the hardcoded CZ set and its stale comment are gone); the amount prints the bare number when the chip carries the unit and `"1890 CZK"` without a market |
| `/plus`, home Plus teaser, wizard Plus step, mobile Subscribe screens | `Membership/GetPlans?countryId=<market>`; price labelled from the response's `CurrencyCode` (ADR-0059) |
| Subscribe / checkout | the command carries the market's `CountryId`; the subscription is created in that currency for life (ADR-0059) |
| `/rewards`, mobile Rewards | the tier floor (`LoyaltyTierConfig.MinimumOrderAmountForDiscount`) is a **platform-default-currency** number enforced only on default-currency orders — rendered **only when the market's currency is the default**, with that code; otherwise the floor line is omitted. With no market resolved the web and Android render the line, iOS keeps the catalogue-default label |
| Copy figures (rules card, T&C §3, mobile trust badge, FAQ) | interpolated from `MarketListItem` (ADR-0060) |
| Recurring form (mobile) | before an address is picked the catalogue is read for the chosen market; a failed catalogue read on Android is a `RecurringCatalogState.Error` with a retry and submit disabled, not an empty list; iOS edit mode prunes the template against its market's catalogue on first load |
| Market selector (navbar/footer on web; Profile → Preferences on mobile) | lists `Market/GetOverview`; writes D3's store |

**Does NOT read it:**

- **Partner** app/host — a cleaner's currency is the work country's (`ResolveCurrencyForEmployeeAsync`).
- **Admin** — reports take an explicit `CurrencyId`; forms author every active currency.
- **An existing order, dispute, credit account, invoice** — each carries its own `CurrencyId`.
- **The wizard after the address step**, the web recurring form, an order-again flow — address wins
  (D4), and before an address is chosen all three recurring forms read the chosen market.
- **My membership** — the subscription's own currency (ADR-0059), which may differ from the market
  chosen later.
- **The order payment sheets' `countryCode`** (Android `GooglePayConfiguration.countryCode = "CZ"` on
  the booking and order-detail sheets) — the *merchant's* country per Stripe, not the customer's. The
  **Plus** sheet on Android is the one exception: it reads `market.isoAlpha2` and falls back to `"CZ"`
  (see §Consequences).
- **The server** — no header, no session field; the market reaches the API only as `countryId`.

### D6 — The chip (R3) and the selector: one shared component per client, drawn once

- **Web:** `<cleansia-market-switcher variant="pill" | "chip">` — one component, two variants, both
  wrapping the same `p-select`, the `variant` pattern the language switcher already has. The pill sits
  after the language pill in the navbar (desktop and the mobile-menu row `nav.market`) and the footer,
  with a map glyph where the language pill has a globe, and is **drawn only with two or more markets**.
  The quick-quote chip is a `.cl-chip`: a **control** with two or more markets, a **static label**
  (`<span class="cl-chip cl-chip--static">`, no chevron, not focusable) with one, absent with none —
  it always names the unit of the quote beside it. Rows read `{translated name} · {currencyCode}`; no
  flag anywhere.
- **Android / iOS:** `MarketScreen` / `MarketPickerView`, a radio-row preference beside Language, and
  a home-tab header chip (`CleansiaChip` geometry, button semantics — Android's component gained a
  `role` parameter, iOS draws an inline twin with `.isButton` only) that opens the same picker on tap.
  **Both the chip and the Preferences row render only with two or more markets** — the mobile home has
  no price beside the chip to label, so a static chip there would be a unit with no number (design
  pass D-3). Today, with one market, the mobile home and Preferences are pixel-identical to the tree
  before this programme.
- **Changing the market** re-dispatches every reader in D5 with the new `countryId`; the mobile
  `CatalogRepository.refresh(countryId)` / `CatalogClient.loadCatalog(countryId:)` already took it.

### D7 — Expansion story: switching a market on is data, in this order, with two gates

1. `Currency` row exists (admin) with a **`LoyaltyPointsDivisor`** (the existing `ActivateCurrency`
   gate, `currency.loyalty_divisor_missing`) and is **priced**: `ServicePrices` / `PackagePrices` /
   `ExtraPrices` (the admin forms require every *active* currency, and allow an inactive one — price
   first);
2. pay coverage in that currency (`EmployeePayConfig` rows, platform-wide);
3. `ActivateCurrency` (the market switch) — **gate 1**, existing: refuses without the divisor;
4. `CountryConfiguration` row for the country with `DefaultCurrencyCode` = that code (**seed SQL only**
   today — no admin writer exists beyond the one field ADR-0060 adds; named gap, not in this
   programme); the country's `IsoAlpha2` (seeded for every row; required on the admin create form);
5. `MembershipPlanPrices` rows for every plan in that currency (ADR-0059) — optional: without them
   Plus is simply not on sale in that market;
6. market content for the country (ADR-0060: `NoShowCredit` on the currency, `InsuranceCoverageAmount`
   on the country) — optional: without it the no-figure copy variants render;
7. `PUT api/AdminCountry/{id}/serviced` — **gate 2, new in this programme:** `SetCountryServiced`
   with `IsServiced = true` refuses `country.market_not_ready` unless the country has a
   `CountryConfiguration` whose `DefaultCurrencyCode` names an **active** currency. Turning a country
   **off** is never gated. On success the country appears in `Market/GetOverview`, the selector lists
   it, the chip can show it.

**Why gate 2 exists.** `Country/GetServiced` feeds the wizard's address step on every client. Without
the gate, a serviced-but-unconfigured country is offered as an address, and the quote then throws in
`ResolveCurrencyForCountryAsync` (no configuration) or refuses as not offerable (inactive currency) —
a customer-visible dead end that the admin created three screens earlier. The gate moves the refusal
to the admin who can act on it. `Market/GetOverview`'s "omit and log" branch (D1) stays as the
backstop for seed-authored states the gate never saw. **Rejected — gating `ActivateCurrency` on a
serviced country instead:** the currency is activated *before* the country is serviced (order above),
so that gate would fire on the wrong step.

Nothing in 1–7 is code now that the gate has shipped. Switching SK or PL on is **not** in this
programme (mechanism only). → /architecture/platform-expandability#expansion-path

## Alternatives considered

| # | Alternative | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Keep "market = the booking's address"; no selector | The owner ruled R-market. Also leaves the Plus page, the copy and the home catalogue with no market at all before an address, which is where R1 and R2 need one. |
| A2 | A **currency** selector | The customer picks a *country*; the currency follows. SK and DE share EUR but not copy, presets or legal text. |
| A3 | Infer the market from Accept-Language / device region | A locale is not a market (Ukrainian-speaking customers in Prague). Also needs its own SSR cache key and `Vary`. |
| A4 | Store the choice on the `User` and sync | No server reader (D3). |
| A5 | Store `Country.Id` in the cookie | Reseeds re-mint ULIDs (D3). |
| A6 | Default market = `CompanyInfo.CountryId` | Tenant-scoped read on an anonymous path; can disagree with the default currency (D2). |
| A7 | Only show the web quote chip when ≥ 2 markets | The chip is also the label of the quote's currency; hiding it hides the unit. It degrades to a static label with one market (D6). The **mobile** header chip is the opposite case — no number beside it — and does hide. |
| A8 | Throw from `Market/GetOverview` when ≥ 2 markets share the default currency | It is a legal state (EUR default + SVK + DEU), and the endpoint is the anonymous landing page's; a throw is a 500 on `/`. Deterministic tiebreak + error log (D2). |
| A9 | `CountryConfiguration.IsDefaultMarket` flag now | No admin writer for `CountryConfiguration` exists; a second source with nothing to keep it aligned. Deferred until two markets share the default currency (D2). |
| A10 | localStorage + cookie on the web (the language pair) | The two stores disagree after cookie expiry and the browser branch would read the wrong one → hydration mismatch (D3). |
| A11 | No gate on `SetCountryServiced` | The wizard offers an address the quote cannot price; the refusal lands on the customer instead of the admin (D7). |
| A12 | Print `CZE · CZK` on the chip, or a per-client alpha-3 → alpha-2 map | The owner wrote "CZ · CZK"; a column on `Country` is one source, three client maps are three that drift (D1, Q-DESIGN-01). |

## Consequences

- **Docs revised:** `platform-expandability.md` now says the market of an **order** is the address's
  country and the market a customer **browses** in is chosen, defaulting to the default market; "no
  currency picker" became "no *currency* picker — a *market* selector, whose currency follows";
  `business-rules.md` likewise, and `#money-constants` lists "promotion moves the default market".
- **Role card:** `/domain/roles/market-directory`.
- **Clients:** new preference + selector + chip on three clients; every D5 reader passes `countryId`
  when a market resolved, and none when the list could not be loaded (D3). Frozen mobile wire rosters
  moved where ADR-0059/0060 add fields.
- **Admin:** `SetCountryServiced(true)` gained the D7 gate and `country.market_not_ready`; the
  country form gained the alpha-2 (required on create, pattern-checked and omitted-when-blank on
  edit, upper-cased on save); `country.iso_alpha2_invalid` joined the admin `api.*` catalogue ×5.
- **The partner app reads the alpha-2 too.** `CountryListItem` carries `isoAlpha2`, and the Android
  partner app's service-area data source now reads it off the wire for its Mapbox-facing country code,
  keeping its device-side alpha-3 → alpha-2 map only as the fallback for a row that arrives without
  one. That is a reader of the column, not of the market.
- **Deviations from the draft, recorded:**
  - *Mobile chip visibility.* The draft had the chip "always render"; the design pass ruled the mobile
    header chip and Preferences row render only with two or more markets (D6). The web quote chip
    always renders when a market resolved.
  - *Android Google Pay on the Plus sheet.* The draft's NOT list left every payment sheet's
    `countryCode` at the merchant's `"CZ"`. The Android lane briefly set the **Plus** sheet's to the
    market's alpha-2; Stripe documents `countryCode` as the *merchant's* country, so it was put back to
    `"CZ"` the same night. The sheet's `currencyCode` is the selected plan's, which is the change that
    matters.
  - *Empty directory on mobile* is treated as the unavailable state (no chip, no `countryId`), which
    the draft did not spell out.
- **The SSR transfer cache is dead for the customer app, so the browser refetches.** D3's hydration
  invariant holds — same cookie, same list, same URLs on both branches — but the draft's "the browser

  sent `withCredentials`, and `CustomerAuthInterceptorFn` marks every own-API request that way. Every
  SSR page therefore refetches on hydrate against an empty store. Not this programme's to fix (a
  reported finding on the interceptor); the market is not the cause and is not worse off than the
  catalogue.
- **Cost accepted:** a signed-in customer re-picks on a new device (defaults to CZ). The default
  market is a tiebreak until an explicit flag exists (D2).

## How a reviewer verifies compliance

1. `Market/GetOverview` exists on **both** customer hosts, is `[AllowAnonymous]`, and lists exactly the
   countries satisfying D1 — `GetMarketsHandlerTests` seed a serviced country with no configuration and
   assert it is absent and logged; seed two default-currency markets and assert **one** `IsDefault`
   (lowest ISO code) and an error log, never a throw; un-service the default currency's country and
   assert no `IsDefault` and an error log. `MarketDirectoryRouteTests` read it anonymously end to end.
2. No customer surface calls `GetOverview`, `QuoteOrder`, `GetPlans` or a subscribe command **without**
   `countryId` once a market has resolved (the web facade specs assert the argument; Android
   `MembershipRepositoryTest` / `HomeTabViewModelTest`; iOS `MembershipViewModelTests` /
   `HomeTabViewModelTests`), and every one of them sends none when `Market/GetOverview` failed (one
   spec per client on the failure branch: no chip, no persisted value, labels from payloads).
3. Web: `preferred_market` cookie is the **only** store (`grep localStorage` in

5. Android/iOS: the preference is in the settings store beside `language` (`MarketRepositoryTest`,

## Challenge / Defense / Verdict

### Challenge (Architect, challenger mode, 2026-09-13)

1. **D2 threw on a legal state.** "Two serviced countries on the default currency" is the ordinary
   state once EUR is the default and SVK + DEU are both on; the throw sat on the anonymous read that
   feeds `/`. *Conceded:* D2 rewritten — deterministic tiebreak + error log, never a throw; the
   explicit flag is deferred with its reason; Q-MARKET-01 asks the owner.
2. **D3's localStorage + cookie pair was a hydration mismatch waiting for a cookie expiry.** The
   language's browser branch reads localStorage, not the cookie; copying that pair for the market
   makes the client resolve a different market than the server rendered when the two stores disagree.
   *Conceded:* cookie is the only web store; both branches read it; the language pair is a reported
   finding.
3. **D3 named no failure mode for the bootstrap dependency on `Market/GetOverview`.** A stored ISO
   code needs the list to become an id; an SSR render that awaits an API call needs to say what it
   renders when that call fails. *Conceded:* the no-market shape is specified (no `countryId`, no
   chip, payload labels, nothing persisted, SSR must not fail).
4. **Cookie hygiene unstated.** The value is attacker-controlled text and was to be "mapped";
   nothing said it is never rendered or sent. *Conceded:* compare-only rule added.
5. **D7 listed no gate on the country side.** `Country/GetServiced` feeds the wizard's address step;
   a serviced country whose currency is inactive or unconfigured is a customer-visible dead end.
   *Conceded:* `SetCountryServiced(true)` gains `country.market_not_ready`; the divisor gate on
   `ActivateCurrency` is named in the order.
6. **The Plus offer inside a booking priced in another currency was unspecified** — D4 rule 1 (address
   wins) and R-market (the subscription follows the market) both claimed it. *Conceded:* the Plus
   step follows the chosen market; the savings figure follows the basket; stated with the reason.
7. **"Works for anonymous visitors" was asserted for three clients; both mobile apps gate on
   sign-in.** *Conceded:* anonymous is web-only, stated; the mobile chip opens the picker so the
   control is one tap from the price.

### Defense

Everything above was conceded and revised in place; no challenge was rebutted. Two items were
carried rather than settled here: the explicit default-market flag (Q-MARKET-01) and whether the
mobile home chip should be a control at all when one market is listed (the design pass).
