> **One decision:** *what the platform writes down so that the contract for work the lawyer's model
> says forms at the cleaner's acceptance is a record and not an inference — on both sides.* Today the
> customer's booking stores a terms *version string* in a three-year audit payload and the order row
> carries no legal text at all; the take writes a seat the next drop hard-deletes and nothing that
> says which text the cleaner agreed to, what the job was when they agreed, or that they agreed at
> all. The text the order is booked under, the cleaner's acceptance of that text, the acts that write
> it, what each party sees, and the record's survival are one contract: a row nobody can find, a row
> naming a text only one party saw, or a row the platform can write without the cleaner's own act is
> the same failure — a claim against the cleaner the platform cannot substantiate.

## Decision

### D0 — The rule

**An order is booked under a stored, versioned contract-for-work text, stamped on the order at
booking; a cleaner is on a seat because they accepted that text for that seat — the exact text row
they read, with the job facts they were shown frozen beside it — or, when an administrator put them
there, they accept it before they start or finish; and both parties can read what was accepted.**

The text is the platform's (ADR-0063: seeded, dated, immutable in force) and it is **fixed per order
at the offer**, not at the acceptance — a contract's terms are those the offer was made under. The
acceptance is the cleaner's act (S1: the subject is the session, never a client field). The facts are
the server's (ADR-0062 D3). The row is append-only and outlives the seat, the drop, the order's
anonymisation and the person (ADR-0062 D5).

### D1 — The document: `LegalDocumentType.WorkContract`, **customer audience**, stamped on the order at booking

```
LegalDocumentType.WorkContract = 2                              -- Cleansia.Core.Domain/Legal/LegalDocumentType.cs
Seed/Legal/customer/work-contract/{ISO3|any}/{yyyy-MM-dd}/{cs,en,sk,uk,ru}.md

Order
  + WorkContractDocumentId  varchar(26)?  FK LegalDocuments (Restrict)   -- the text this job was offered under; set once, by OrderFactory
  IX (WorkContractDocumentId)
```

- **Customer audience, not `Employee` (C1, the panel's reversal of the author's D1).** The contract
  is between the customer and the cleaner; its terms are, by the lawyer's own sentence, *"podpůrně …
  Obchodními podmínkami Operátora pro Zákazníky"* — the customer terms. A text the cleaner accepts
  that the customer never could read before booking is a counter-offer, not an acceptance
  (Civil Code § 1740(2)); two texts, one per party, is no consensus on terms. The audience enum is
  *who a text is published to before they are bound*: the customer is bound at booking, so the text
  is published where the customer's texts are — `/work-contract` beside `/terms` and `/privacy`
  (one two-line component in the `legal-pages` lib, `terms.component.html:1-4` is the shape; the
  route; the two mobile customer wizards link to it, ADR-0063 D9's rule) — and referenced from the
  wizard's confirm step by the lawyer's P074 sentence (D6). The cleaner reads the same text through
  the order (D3/D4) by id, which no audience filters. **Falls out for free:** `LegalDocumentResolver`
  needs no audience parameter (C2 is void), the parity checker's root stays the customer folder,
  ADR-0063 D9's *"no employee-audience document"* stands.
- **One new enum member and one folder name.** `LegalSeedResource.Types["work-contract"]`
  (`LegalSeedResource.cs:31-35`) and the parse error's path text; `LEGAL_SEED_TYPES` gains
  `work-contract` (`check-booking-policy-parity.mjs:55`) so a baked `1 000 Kč` in the contract is a
  red build like it is in the terms; `TYPE_LABEL_KEYS` in the admin legal-documents page gains the
  entry **in the same commit as the regenerated admin client** (it is an exhaustive `Record`;
  `legal-documents.models.ts:17-20`). `LegalDocument.TypeFor(ConsentType)` is untouched — the
  acceptance is not a `UserConsent` (D2).
- **Stamped on the order at booking: `Order.WorkContractDocumentId`.** `OrderFactory.CreateAsync`
  resolves `ILegalDocumentResolver.ResolveInForceAsync(WorkContract, input.Address.CountryId)` before
  pricing — beside the pay-coverage gate at `OrderFactory.cs:63-73` and for the same reason: an order
  no contract can form on must not be booked, and the recurring materializer reaches the factory
  without `CreateOrder`'s validator — and throws `InvalidOperationException` when nothing is in force
  (a deploy with a future-dated-only folder; the seed is embedded, runs at every host start and falls
  back to `any`, so the seed test in Verification #2 is the guard and a validator rule for it is
  machinery for a case CI already catches — CLAUDE.md §4). `order.SetWorkContractDocument(document)`
  beside `order.SetCurrency(...)` at `:164`; private setter, set once.
- **Why the column is nullable, and what that does not mean.** `Order.Create(` has 153 callers,
  152 of them tests; a required parameter or a NOT NULL column touches every one and every
  integration fixture that inserts an order. The production writer is one and always stamps it
  (`OrderFactoryWorkContractStampTests` pins that); the take (D3) treats an order without one as an
  order no text can match — refused, never resolved lazily. A null is a fixture, not a state.
- **Resolved by the *order's* market** (`Address.CountryId`), with ADR-0063 D4's rule — the market's
  own copy beats the platform-wide one, then newest. The contract governs a job at an address.
- **The first version is the merge date, market `any`, five languages**, with the draft line the
  customer texts carry (`…/2026-09-14/en.md:5`) as the first paragraph in every language and
  `{{currency}}` where a price is mentioned (ADR-0063 D5). The lawyer's final wording is a new dated
  folder plus a deploy (ADR-0063 D3/D7); **a new version applies to orders booked from its date** and
  changes nothing on an order already booked — which is the legally right behaviour and the reason
  there is no "stale version" race anywhere below.
- **The text is a template.** It may say *"the job identified by the order number, date, time window,
  price and location shown to you at acceptance"* and may use `{{currency}}`; it may **not** carry a
  per-job figure, a name, an address or a date — the job facts bind through the snapshot on the
  acceptance row (D2), never through a generated per-job text (a `LegalDocument` per order is
  unversionable, unhashable and outside seed-plus-deploy). `{{currency}}` is filled from
  **`Order.Currency.Code`** on the preview and the read (C12), from the market on the public page as
  today.


- **Private setters, one static `Create(...)`, one mutator `Pseudonymise()`** that nulls `IpAddress`,
  `DeviceLabel`, `DeviceId` and nothing else — the `CustomerActionAudit` discipline (ADR-0062 D5),
  enforced by a reflection test in the `CustomerActionAuditImmutabilityTests` shape.
- **`TenantAuditable`.** Written by an authenticated cleaner in a request scope: `CommitAsync` stamps
  `CreatedBy` (the cleaner's user id — server truth beside `EmployeeId`), `CreatedOn` and `TenantId`
  (the ambient tenant: the cleaner's company, which is the order's operator because the take reads
  the order through the filter). `EmployeeActionAudit.cs:57-61` gives the same reason.
- **Bound to the seat, not to (order, employee) (C5).** The seat id is the one fact that names *this*
  assignment and no other: a take → drop → re-take is two seats and two rows; a take → drop → admin
  re-add of the same cleaner is a new seat with **no** row, so the gate (D3) refuses exactly the case
  the author's draft had to concede as a residual. The seat is hard-deleted, so the column is a bare
  scalar the way `EmployeeActionAudit.EmployeeId` is; the row is history and does not need the seat
  to exist. `UNIQUE (OrderEmployeeId)` is both the legal truth (one contract per seat) and the
  database's answer to a concurrent double accept (C13) — the author's "no unique index" reasoning
  was about `(OrderId, EmployeeId)`, which would have forced the re-take to rewrite the first
  contract; per seat, nothing is ever rewritten.
- **The text row, not the document plus a language (C4).** ADR-0041 D2.2's argument is exactly this:
  a Czech reader and a Ukrainian reader of one version agreed to two different strings, and the record
  must say which. One FK gives document, version, language and `ContentHash` by join, so ADR-0041
  D3.3's *"a silent edit is a one-query detection"* comes free. A client-supplied `Language` would
  also have been a second `RuleFor` on `TakeOrder`'s validator, which must stay one chain
  (`TakeOrder.cs:40-47`, ADR-0037 D6) — one string member, no second chain.
- **Why not a `UserConsent`.** One row per (user, type), overwritten on regrant, keyed on the user,
  resolved from the user's market, no column for the facts. ADR-0041 D1's answer for the self-billing
  agreement; this is the per-job twin.
- **Why not a column on `OrderEmployee`.** `Order.UnassignEmployee` hard-deletes the seat row —
  load-bearing for the seat index (`EmployeeActionAudit.cs:22-25`) — so a stamp on the seat dies with
  the first drop, which is precisely the case a claim turns on.
- **Why a snapshot at all, when the order row exists.** The order row is anonymised by the erasure
  and the two-year sweep and its state moves on (`CompletedAt`, a refund, an admin override); a future
  order-edit command (VOP 3.3 promises one) would change the scope under an acceptance already given.
  The snapshot is *what the cleaner was shown when they swiped*; the order row is *what the job
  became*. The facts are built by **one builder with its own `AsNoTracking` projection**
  (`WorkContractFactsBuilder.BuildAsync(orderId)`) shared by the preview and the acceptor — never off
  the take handler's tracked aggregate, whose includes (`TakeOrder.cs:261-268`) are the seat-race
  arbiter's and must not widen (C11).
- **Never the full address, never the customer's name.** *(a)* At the instant of acceptance the
  cleaner has not been shown the street or the name (`OrderPiiRedaction.cs:41-68`); a record claiming
  *"you accepted a job at Vinohradská 12"* would record a fact they never saw. *(b)* The row outlives
  the person; a street in it is a second, uncontrolled copy no erasure walk reaches (ADR-0012 D4.1).
  The customer's identity resolves **through the order** at render time and reads as the anonymised
  marker after erasure, as the incident file's does. `WorkContractFactsPiiGuardTests` walks every
  `IWorkContractFacts` member name (recursively) against the archive guard's token list
  (`adr-0064.md:731-735`) so a `customerName` "because the contract should name the parties" is a red
  build before it is a review comment.
- **The coarse location is a term of the contract, kept on purpose, and it is personal data in coarse
  form (C12, stated rather than denied).** `Address.Anonymize()` blanks `City` and `ZipCode` at the
  source (`Address.cs:57-59`); `locationApproximate` is a permanent coarse copy — *"Praha · 120"* —
  because the *místo plnění* is a term of the contract and it is the pre-acceptance disclosure the
  board already makes, produced by the same builder so the screen and the row cannot disagree. It is
  kept under the same ground as the rest of the row (D5: Art. 6(1)(b), then 17(3)(e)). **Q-WC-07**
  asks the lawyer/DPO to confirm; the default is to keep it.

### D3 — The acts that write it: `TakeOrder` inline; `AcceptWorkContract` after an admin placement; `StartOrder` **and** `CompleteOrder` gate

**The command member.** `TakeOrder.Command(string OrderId, string? AcceptedWorkContractTextId)`
(`TakeOrder.cs:18`). **The text-row id is the tick**: `null` is *not accepted*; a value is *"I read
this text and I accept it"*. This replaces orchestrator default (2)'s `bool` (proves a tap, not a text)
and the author's `(documentId, language)` pair (C4) with ADR-0041 D3's echo of the served row.

**The validator** — two rules in the one ordered chain (`TakeOrder.cs:48-73`, ADR-0037 D6):

| Position | Rule | Key | Why there |
|---|---|---|---|
| after `Required`, **before** `ExistsAndIsOpenToCallerAsync` | `AcceptedWorkContractTextId` not blank | `contract.not_accepted` | Depends on nothing about the order, so it cannot leak existence or the hold; every shipped client sends it, so it fires only on a broken or stale client. |
| after `NotHaveTimeConflictAsync` (**last**) | the id names a text of **the order's** `WorkContractDocumentId` | `contract.text_mismatch` | Depends on the order, so after existence; last because every refusal ahead of it is a better answer (*"this job is gone"* beats *"wrong text"*), and TC-TAKE-ONE-ERROR still holds. An order with no document (a fixture) matches no text and is refused here. |

There is **no stale-version case** (D1: the order's document is fixed at booking), so the key says
what is true: the echoed text is not this order's. A mismatch is a client that cached an id from
another order or rendered a text of another document; the client contract on it is ADR-0041 D3.5's
loop (re-run the preview, re-render, reset the gesture — D6). `legal.document_not_found` reaches the
partner surface through the **preview only** (an order with no document), never through the take.

**The handler.** Between `order.AddAssignedEmployee(orderEmployee)` (`TakeOrder.cs:312`) and
`orderRepository.CommitAsync` (`:331`):

```csharp
await workContractAcceptor.StageAsync(order, orderEmployee, command.AcceptedWorkContractTextId!, cancellationToken);
```

The seat, the status row, the acceptance and its audit row are **one transaction**; the seat-race and
cover-race losers (`:333-360`) roll all four back and no acceptance exists for a seat that was never
won. The notifications after `:331` are untouched. The handler gains **one** collaborator (six to
seven); the acceptor absorbs the rest.

**`WorkContractAcceptor.StageAsync(order, seat, textId)`** (`Services/WorkContractAcceptor.cs`) — the
one place the row is built (two callers today, CLAUDE.md §4): load the text row with its document,
guard `text.LegalDocumentId == order.WorkContractDocumentId` (the validator is the gate; the acceptor
throws `InvalidOperationException` on a mismatch it should never see), `WorkContractFactsBuilder.BuildAsync(order.Id)`,
`IRequestMetadataProvider.{IpAddress, DeviceLabel}`, **`IUserSessionProvider.GetTypedUserClaim(AuthExtensions.DeviceIdClaimType)`**
for the device (C7), `IHostAudienceProvider.Audience`, `WorkContractAcceptance.Create(...)`,
`acceptanceRepository.Add`, and `employeeActionAuditRepository.Add(EmployeeActionAudit.Create(seat.EmployeeId, order.Id, EmployeeAuditAction.ContractAccepted))`
— the row is the evidence, the audit row its index (D5). It does **not** commit.

**The preview: `GetWorkContractPreview.Query(OrderId, Language)`** (partner hosts, `[Permission
CanTakeOrder]`) → `WorkContractDto(LegalDocumentTextId, LegalDocumentId, Version, EffectiveFrom,
Language, Title, ContentHtml, Facts, Acceptance: null)` — the **order's** document
(`order.WorkContractDocumentId`, `legal.document_not_found` when null), `TextForOrFallback(language)`
of it, `ContentHtml` through `LegalMarkdownRenderer.Render` with `{{currency}}` = the order's currency
code, the facts from the one builder. The order must be open to the caller exactly as the take
requires it (`OrderVisibility.OpenTo`, `TakeOrder.cs:90-101`; a held order is a missing order to
everyone but its beneficiary; an assigned cleaner passes through `PayableTo`'s crew term) —
`order.not_found` otherwise.

**The admin path.** `AdminReassignOrder` (`AdminReassignOrder.cs:122-123`) is **unchanged**: it
writes no acceptance — an administrator cannot accept a contract on the cleaner's behalf, and a row
written there would be *"the platform asserting an act the cleaner never performed into an append-only
legal log"* (`adr-0041.md:1632-1633`). The cleaner is already told they were assigned (`:144-145`);
the push copy is unchanged (the job detail carries the prompt, D6); the **admin** sees the pending
state on the order detail (D4, C9). Whether an admin placement should instead become an *offer* the
cleaner takes (ADR-0036's preferred hold, so that every seat is formed by `TakeOrder` and the gates
below are deleted) is **Q-WC-06** — an owner question about whether an admin may force a crew member
at all, not a default the panel takes.

**The standalone act: `AcceptWorkContract.Command(OrderId, AcceptedWorkContractTextId)`** (partner
hosts, `[Permission CanTakeOrder]`, an `ICommand` the UnitOfWork commits). One chain: `Required` →
blank id → `contract.not_accepted` → `ExistsAsync` → `order.not_found` → the caller holds a seat on
the crew → `order.employee_not_assigned` → the order is **not over** — `CurrentStatus ∈
OrderAvailability.OfferableStatuses` (`OrderAvailability.cs:53-54`: `New | Confirmed | OnTheWay |
InProgress`; `take_order.already_cancelled` / `take_order.already_completed` reused) — so a cleaner an
admin placed on an **in-progress** job can accept (C3: the author's `Confirmed | OnTheWay` restriction
dead-ended exactly that cleaner) → the id names a text of the order's document →
`contract.text_mismatch`. Handler: a row for **this seat** already exists → `Success`, no row (the
double tap; ADR-0041 D2's one sanctioned success-without-a-row); else the acceptor stages the row and
its audit row. Not pipeline-audited (employee acts land nowhere, ADR-0062 D1). **A concurrent double
tap** — two requests both reading "no row" — is arbitrated by `UNIQUE (OrderEmployeeId)`: the loser's
pipeline commit fails and answers a 500 whose retry is a success. Stated, not built around (C13): a
handler-level commit-with-catch is `TakeOrder`'s exception for a seat race, not the norm for a
double tap.

**The gates.** `HasAcceptedWorkContractForSeatAsync` — *an acceptance row whose `OrderEmployeeId` is
the caller's current seat on this order* — under **`contract.acceptance_required`**, on:

- **`StartOrder`** (`StartOrder.cs:49-58`, the second chain) **after** `EmployeeIsAssignedToOrderAsync`
  (a non-assignee still answers `order.employee_not_assigned` and learns nothing) and **before**
  `EmployeeHasNoOrderInProgressAsync`.
- **`CompleteOrder`** (`CompleteOrder.cs:83-90`, the employee chain) **after**
  `EmployeeIsAssignedToOrderAsync` (`:85-86`) (C3). The act that ends the work is the last act that
  can still have a contract behind it, and it is the one a reklamace turns on; on a two-seat crew the
  second cleaner never starts (the order is already `InProgress`) but may complete.

Two keys for one fact, deliberately: `contract.not_accepted` means *the client did not send a text*
(a bug, shown as an error); `contract.acceptance_required` means *you have a contract to accept* (a
product state the app answers by opening the contract sheet). `NotifyOnTheWay` is **not** gated —
travel is not the work.

**The residual, stated.** A second crew member who neither starts nor completes — placed by an admin
on a crew whose other member does both — works under no acceptance. The job-detail banner, the Start
and Complete refusals for anyone who does tap, and the admin's pending line (D4) are the nudges; the
structural close is Q-WC-06. Accepted, not built around.

**Drop, cover, rejection, reassignment leave the row.** The seat goes; the acceptance stays — it is
history, and the pair *accepted at 09:00 / dropped at 09:40* is the record a claim is answered from.
**The cover swap writes no employee-side row of its own (C6, corrected):** `DropOrder.cs:150` and
`RequestCover.cs:123` are the only `EmployeeActionAudit` writers; a displaced cleaner's record reads
*accepted (seat S) / cover requested / [the taker's acceptance of the seat with S's ordinal, later]*
— the displacement is inferable from the successor's row, and an enum member for it would record an
event the displaced cleaner did not perform, against `EmployeeAuditAction`'s own definition
(`EmployeeAuditAction.cs:4-13`). Not added.

### D4 — What each party sees, and the rendering

**On the order detail DTO** (`OrderItem`, beside `AssignedEmployees` at `OrderItem.cs:77`):

```
WorkContractAcceptances : IEnumerable<WorkContractAcceptanceDto>
    WorkContractAcceptanceDto(string Id, string OrderEmployeeId, string EmployeeId, DateTime AcceptedOn, string DocumentVersion, string Language)
```

one per **current seat** that has a row (`OrderEmployeeId ∈ AssignedEmployees.Id`); the client pairs
it with the crew entry by `OrderEmployeeId == AssignedEmployeeDto.Id` and takes the name from there —
already audience-masked (`OrderMappers.cs:377-381`), so this DTO carries **no name** and no second
masking path. A crew entry with no matching acceptance **is** the pending state. `OrderPiiRedaction.RedactForBrowsingCleaner`
empties it (`OrderPiiRedaction.cs:41-68`; `OrderRedactionSurfaceTests` fails until the member is
covered). `GetOrderDetails.Handler` gains the acceptance repository — its eleventh collaborator on
a handler already past the charter's smell line; reported, not fixed here.

- **The customer's order detail (web, Android, iOS):** per acceptance, *"Contract for work accepted
  by {firstName} on {date}, version {version}"* and **Read the contract** → the rendering below.
  Before any acceptance the line is absent — the crew list already shows who is on the job; a
  *"contract pending"* line would tell the customer about an internal state — not built.
- **The cleaner's job detail (partner web, Android, iOS):** the caller's row is the one whose
  `OrderEmployeeId` is their own crew entry's `Id`. Present → *"You accepted the contract for work on
  {date}, version {version}"* + **Read the contract**. Absent while assigned (the admin case) → a
  banner *"Accept the contract for work before you start"* with the sheet (D6); **Start** and
  **Complete** stay offered and, if tapped first, answer `contract.acceptance_required` by opening
  the same sheet.
- **The admin's order detail (C9):** one line per crew entry — *accepted {date}, v{version}* /
  *contract pending* — with **Read** on an accepted one. The only path to a pending seat is the
  admin's own act; they should not learn it from a phone call.
- **A guest (C10):** `LookupOrder.Response` is its own projection with no crew (`LookupOrder.cs:50-65`)
  and gains no acceptance line; a guest has no contract read, as they have no receipt route. A guest
  read keyed on the confirmation secret is a finding for the PM, not built.

**The rendering: `GetWorkContract.Query(AcceptanceId, Language)`** — one handler, routed on **all
five hosts** → the same `WorkContractDto` with `Acceptance` filled (`AcceptedOn`, `DocumentVersion`,
`AcceptedLanguage`, `OrderEmployeeId`, `EmployeeId`), `Facts` **from the stored `FactsJson`** (not the
live order), and `ContentHtml` of the **accepted text's document** in the requested language when the
document has it (falling back to the accepted text), with the accepted language on the DTO so a page
can say *"accepted in Czech"* when it renders English. Keyed on the acceptance, not on (order,
employee), so the dropped contract of a re-take is readable and no `EmployeeId` travels on the wire
(S1, C5). Access is derived from the row: the row's order exists for the caller
(`IOrderAccessService.OrderExistsForCallerAsync` — owner-scoped for a customer, any order in the
tenant for an admin, `OrderAccessService.cs:38-57`) **and**, when the caller is an employee, the row's
`EmployeeId` is theirs — an ex-crew cleaner keeps reading the contract they accepted; anyone else
answers `order.not_found`. The parties are named at render time: the cleaner by given name
(Q-WC-03), the customer by the order's `CustomerName`, which reads as the anonymised marker after
erasure.

**The PDF is a follow-up ticket, not phase 1** — proportionality (CLAUDE.md §4). *If it is not built
now:* both parties read the accepted text with the frozen facts in-app, and **a dispute bundle is a
stated procedure (C16): the incident file's contracts section (D5: seat, text id, version, language,
instant, client, IP, device, the facts) plus `AdminGetLegalDocument` for that text with its SHA-256
(ADR-0063 D8)** — nothing a dispute needs is missing. *What building it costs:* the legal texts are
markdown and every QuestPDF document renders typed records; a markdown-to-QuestPDF walker is the new
machinery, under a process-wide render lock a per-tap download would hit. Shape for the follow-up:
`IPdfService.GenerateWorkContractPdf(WorkContractDto)` returning `(Bytes, DataSha256)`, a block
renderer over Markdig's AST, one download route per audience beside `DownloadOrderReceipt`. Filed as
a finding for the PM.

- **The export — both subjects.** The cleaner's `GdprExportDto` gains `WorkContractAcceptances`

- **Erasure — the cleaner.** Roster verdict **`AnonymizedInPlace`**: *"The contract record for a
  retained order. Its subject handle is a bare `EmployeeId` that `Employee.Anonymize` keeps, so it is
  pseudonymous without a write; the three request-metadata columns are personal data and are blanked

  `workContractAcceptanceRepository.PseudonymiseForEmployeeAsync(user.Employee.Id, ct)` in

- **Erasure — the customer.** The row names no customer and is reached through `OrderId` only; the
  customer's erasure anonymises the order and leaves the row untouched.
- **Retention — the row: kept as long as the order is kept, which is forever.** ADR-0064 D3: *"No
  row of the company is deleted by this ADR, ever"*; a contract record for a retained order is books.
  Ground: Art. 6(1)(b) for its existence (the row *is* the formation record), Art. 6(1)(f) with
  Art. 17(3)(e) for its survival past erasure — the ground `EmployeeActionAudit` and `OrderEmployeePay`

### D7 — What is NOT built, by name

- **Signi or any e-signature provider.** The row already carries what a qualified-signature envelope
  would reference — the text row (hence its hash), the instant, the actor, the device claim. A
  provider adds `SignatureEnvelopeId` + `SignedOn` and a webhook and changes nothing above. Whether an
  in-app swipe suffices for a B2C contract for work is the lawyer's question (Q-WC-04).
- **A customer signature or a second customer checkbox.** The customer's half is the VOP consent at
  booking plus the stamped document plus the P074 sentence (D1, D6). The VOP wording that
  incorporates the template by reference is the lawyer's (Q-WC-05).
- **A per-job generated text.** D1 says why.
- **A `TermsDocumentId` on the order for the VOP themselves.** The same gap C1 found exists for the
  customer terms (a version string in a three-year payload); it is the platform ↔ customer relation,
  not this contract's, and is **reported as a finding**, not absorbed.
- **The admin placement as an offer** (C3(b)) — Q-WC-06, an owner question; the alternatives table
  carries its trade-off.
- **A `CoverDisplaced` audit member** (C6) — inferable, and against the enum's definition.
- **A per-preview HMAC proving the sheet was opened for this job (C14).** The echo proves *which
  text*, not that *this* session fetched *this* order's preview; a client that caches the text id from
  job A (same document, same language) and takes job B without opening the sheet records a perfect
  row. ~30 lines of signing would close it with no storage; not built — a cleaner-side client that
  lies about having read is the same threat model as a customer ticking without reading, and the
  platform records the act, not the reading (ADR-0045 D13).
- **The PDF** (D4), **a `NotifyOnTheWay` gate**, **a "contract pending" line for the customer**, **a
  scroll-to-end gate**, **a push copy change on admin placement**, **a re-acceptance on a new version

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not |
|---|---|
| **An `Employee`-audience contract text the cleaner accepts; the customer's side is their VOP consent** (the author's D1, orchestrator default 1) | Two parties, two texts, no row that says both assented to one instrument; the cleaner's acceptance of a text the customer never saw is a counter-offer. The resolver could not even reach it (`LegalDocumentResolver.cs:19-20`). Customer audience, stamped on the order, published before booking (D1). |
| **Resolve the in-force document at the take, not at booking** (the author's D3) | A contract's terms are fixed at the offer; a lawyer's new version would otherwise bind an order booked under the old one, and the author needed a whole midnight-UTC stale-version protocol to paper over the race. One column on the order deletes the race and makes the customer's half durable. |
| A `bool ContractAccepted` on the take, the server stamps the text (orchestrator default 2; ADR-0062 D4) | Proves a tap, not a text. The echo of the text row is ADR-0041 D3's shape and costs one string member. |
| Echo `(documentId, language)` and let the server pick the text again (the author's D3) | Weaker than the precedent it cites (ADR-0041 D2.2 names the text row); a client `Language` is a second validator chain on a command that must stay one; a language added by the seeder after the preview could store a text the cleaner never read. The text row id (C4). |
| Key the row on `(OrderId, EmployeeId)` with no unique index; read newest (the author's D2) | Cannot tell a re-take from an admin re-add of the same cleaner (the author's conceded residual); the dropped contract is unreadable; a concurrent double accept writes two rows. The seat id closes all three and `UNIQUE (OrderEmployeeId)` is the legal truth (C5, C13). |
| Unique `(OrderId, EmployeeId)` + move the row on re-take (`UserConsent.AcceptVersion`'s shape) | Rewrites a legal record to look like the later act. Two seats are two contracts. |
| **The admin placement becomes an offer (ADR-0036's preferred hold) so every seat is formed by `TakeOrder`** (C3(b)) | Structurally the cleanest — it deletes `AcceptWorkContract`, both gates, one key on three clients and the sheet's `accept` mode — but it removes the admin's power to force a crew member, leaves a seat offered until the cleaner acts, and needs the hold's lapse and `NotHeldFrom`'s `AssignedEmployees.Any()` term (`OrderVisibility.cs:79`) rethought for a partly crewed order. A product decision: **Q-WC-06**, default (a). |
| Gate `StartOrder` only (the author's D3) | On a two-seat crew the second cleaner never calls it; on an in-progress reassignment nobody does. `CompleteOrder` is the last act with a contract behind it (C3). |
| Gate `NotifyOnTheWay` as well | Travel is not the work; a second refusal for the same state. |
| Restrict `AcceptWorkContract` to `Confirmed \| OnTheWay` (the author's D3) | Dead-ends the cleaner an admin placed on an in-progress job (`InProgress` is offerable). Any not-over status (C3). |
| Generate a per-job contract text | A `LegalDocument` per order: unversionable, unhashable, outside seed-plus-deploy. The snapshot binds the facts; the rendering is per job. |
| A `ContractAcceptedOn` on `OrderEmployee` | Hard-deleted by the first act a claim turns on. |
| A `ConsentType.WorkContract` in `UserConsent` per job | One row per (user, type), overwritten, keyed on the user. ADR-0041 D1's answer. |
| Build ADR-0041's `EmployeeAgreementStatement` and record the job contract as a `Kind` | Per agreement, revocable, with a paper channel; a job contract is per seat, never revoked, never on paper. The two share their reasoning and can share a table the day the agreement is built. |
| Store `DeviceId` (claim) + `ClientAudience` only; no IP/UA, no sweep, no key (C8) | A web acceptance would then carry nothing but the session; every other legal-act record on the platform carries IP+UA and they are read here (incident file, export). The volume argument that makes `UserConsent` sweep-free does not hold per job; ADR-0062 D5's per-row window is reused by reference. |
| Record `IRequestMetadataProvider.DeviceId` (the author's ticket) | The header, on a signed-in act — exactly what ADR-0062 D1 forbids. The claim (C7). |
| An `EmployeeAuditAction.CoverDisplaced` (C6, optional) | Inferable from the successor's acceptance of the same seat ordinal; records an event the displaced cleaner did not perform. |
| Pipeline-audit the two commands | Employee acts land nowhere by ADR-0062 D1's design. One enum member on the existing table reaches every reader. |
| The PDF in phase 1 | The markdown-to-QuestPDF walker is the machinery; the dispute bundle is a stated procedure without it (D4). |
| Keep the trio for the life of the account like `UserConsent` | Volume (D5). |
| NOT NULL `Orders.WorkContractDocumentId` with a required `Order.Create` parameter | 153 call sites, 152 in tests. Nullable, stamped by the one writer, refused by the take when absent (D1). |
| A lazy stamp at the take for an order with no document | Machinery for a state the production writer cannot produce. |
| A per-preview HMAC (C14) | Stated in D7; not built. |
| Web swipe (a dragged slider) | Not a finger; the tick is the platform's legal-act control on the web (Q-WC-02). |
| Link mobile to a web page for the text (ADR-0063 D9) | The swipe must sit under the text; the customer page is authenticated. In-app, one shared HTML view per platform. |

## Roles (CRC — `docs/domain/roles/`)

**`WorkContractAcceptance`** — *one cleaner's acceptance of the contract for work for one seat of one
job: which text row (hence document, version, language, hash), when, from where, and the job facts as
shown at that instant — append-only, pseudonymised on the cleaner's erasure, kept as long as the
order.* Collaborators: `LegalDocumentText` (the text; FK Restrict), `Order` (the job; FK Restrict),
`WorkContractFacts` (the frozen facts), `WorkContractAcceptor` (the one writer),
`IWorkContractAcceptanceRepository` (`Add`, `GetForSeatsAsync`, `AnyForSeatAsync`, `GetByIdAsync`,
`GetByEmployeeIdNoTrackingAsync`, `PseudonymiseForEmployeeAsync`, `PseudonymiseExpiredAsync`, the
archive stream). Does NOT know: the customer (reached through the order at render time); the
cleaner's name or company (ids only); whether the seat still exists (the seat row says; this row is
history); the street (never shown, never stored); whether the document is still the one in force
(it points at the one the order was booked under); the PDF.

**`WorkContractAcceptor`** — *turn "this cleaner accepts text T for this seat of this order" into a
staged acceptance row and its audit index row, refusing to stage a text that is not of the order's
document.* Collaborators: `ILegalDocumentTextRepository` (or the document repository's text read),
`IWorkContractFactsBuilder`, `IRequestMetadataProvider`, `IUserSessionProvider` (the device claim),
`IHostAudienceProvider`, the acceptance and employee-audit repositories. Does NOT know: the seat's
creation (the caller adds it), the commit (the caller's), the client gesture, the customer.

**`WorkContractFactsBuilder`** — *one `AsNoTracking` projection of the job facts a cleaner is shown
before accepting, used by the preview and the acceptor so the screen and the row cannot differ.*
Collaborators: `IOrderRepository`, `OrderMappers.BuildApproximateAddress`. Does NOT know: the cleaner,
the seat, the text, the take handler's tracked aggregate.

**`Order`** gains one fact: *the contract-for-work text this job was offered under* — set once by
`OrderFactory`, read by the preview, the acceptor and the take's last rule.
