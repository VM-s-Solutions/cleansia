# ADR-0076 — An open cleaning seat stops being offered and takeable when its booked slot ends

- **Status:** `accepted` — **2026-10-13, by Mike's ruling.**
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Supersedes:** [ADR-0037](./adr-0037) D1 in part: its time-independent offerability contract.
  Earlier status and money amendments stand; the historical body remains unchanged.
- **Superseded by:** —
- **Applies to:** `OrderAvailability` and its browse, take, dashboard and notification consumers.
- **Ticket:** T-0854. The native Available-tab request change is T-0855.

## Context

On 2026-10-13 Mike answered the question about jobs dated before today: **“I don't think that it needs
to be offerable”**. He then chose the recommended slot-end rule with **“Go with defaults
(recommended)”**: a free seat leaves offer and take at start plus estimated length, as double-booking
measures the slot.

A time-independent rule lets days-old work survive an absent or bounded cancellation sweep. A fixed
two-hour board floor also hides a 180- or 240-minute started slot before its booked end. The 2026-09-06
ruling permits a cleaner to join started work with a free seat; the time limit must preserve that.

## Decision

The existing status and money rule gains one conjunct:

```csharp
order.CleaningDateTime.AddMinutes(order.EstimatedTime) > nowUtc
```

Equality is ended. `EstimatedTime` is the raw booked duration in minutes used by the double-booking
check; crew size does not divide it. SQL and in-memory forms take the supplied UTC instant, and their
agreement is tested against PostgreSQL. The coarse status array and the separate seat, preferred-hold,
currency and cleaner-eligibility terms remain.

The cleaner's open board and dashboard use this end bound instead of their two-hour start-date floor.
An explicit client date filter keeps its purpose. Assigned work is independent of the open-offer arm;
an existing assignment, the owning customer and an administrator still access ended work.

## Consequences

- `OnTheWay` and `InProgress` may have a takeable seat before the raw booked end, including more than
  two hours after start. Browse and take stop at end, and a stale list's take gets `order.not_takeable` when expiry is the first failed check. Earlier opaque existence/hold/currency checks and terminal-status refusals keep their existing keys.
- The unfilled-order sweep keeps its 30-minute grace and 168-hour lookback, its never-started/empty-crew
  selection and its money behavior. A late unfilled slot can be taken before that sweep gives up,
  while its booked slot remains open. The end bound does not wait for the sweep.
- Preferred, new-order and seat-open notices also use the same rule and supplied instant. The digest
  keeps its additional future-only selection.
- Administrative reassignment and cover/drop status gates retain their purpose. This decision adds no
  wire field, error key, schema change, client regeneration or migration.

## Verification

Fixed-time tests cover before, at and after the end, started work and payment qualification. The
PostgreSQL agreement fixture checks SQL and memory against expected row ids, both Available request
shapes, dashboard count and preview, browse, contract preview and take. The 180/240-minute started rows
at +130 minutes pin the removed display-floor conflict. Seat-open tests pin no recipient query or
notice at/after end. Existing completed-dashboard and overlap plan tests retain their distinct scope.
