This complaints procedure describes how you complain about a cleaning ordered through the Cleansia mobile apps or the Cleansia website, how your complaint is decided and how a justified complaint is put right. It completes the Terms of Service and does not affect your statutory rights.

The company is responsible to you for the proper performance of the cleaning and for damage caused while performing it, including the work of the cleaners it engages. You complain to the company, not to the cleaner; the company deals with the cleaner.

## 2. What you can complain about

- The cleaning was not done properly.
- Part of the booked cleaning was not done.
- The cleaning did not take place.
- Something was damaged during the cleaning.
- You were charged something you did not agree to, or a wrong amount.
- Anything else about the service you booked.

You can complain about a cleaning once its scheduled start has passed. Before that, you can cancel the booking under the terms (section 13).

If the assigned cleaner has not arrived after the start time, report it in the app or on the website as a cleaning that did not take place, or as a guest through the link in the booking e-mail. Our staff verify it; when they confirm it, the booking is cancelled as described in the terms (section 14) — you pay nothing and a card payment is refunded in full. If one of several cleaners did not arrive, it is settled as a complaint under this procedure.

## 3. How to file a complaint

With an account, open the booking in the app or on the website and report a problem. Choose what went wrong, describe it and, if it concerns only part of the booking, say which services. Attach photos if you can: they are the best evidence. Choose how a justified complaint should be settled (section 6).

As a guest, send your complaint by e-mail to {{companyEmail}} with the booking number, a description of what went wrong and, if you can, photos.

Your complaint and the date you filed it are shown in the app and on the website. You and our staff can then exchange messages about it, and you can add photos.

While a complaint about a booking is open, add to it rather than filing a new one. Once it has been decided, you can file another complaint about the same booking.

## 4. When to complain

Report a problem as soon as possible. A complaint within 24 hours of the end of the cleaning — or of its scheduled start, if it did not take place — is on time, and the company then undertakes to put a justified complaint right.

A later complaint is still accepted and decided on its merits, and your statutory rights for defective performance are not affected. The sooner you complain, the easier it is to establish what happened: the cleaner's photos of your home are deleted 7 days after the cleaning, unless a complaint about it is open by then.

## 5. How your complaint is decided

Our staff review your description and photos, the photos the cleaner took before and after the cleaning, the record of the booking and, where needed, the cleaner's account of what happened. They may ask you for more information through the complaint. The complaint shows its current state, such as under review, waiting for your reply or resolved.

We decide your complaint without undue delay and at the latest within 30 days of receiving it, unless we agree a longer period with you. You receive the decision with its reasons in the app and on the website; as a guest, by e-mail.

## 6. How a justified complaint is put right

A justified complaint is normally put right by refunding the price of the part that was not done properly, or the whole price if the cleaning did not take place. Damage caused while performing the cleaning is compensated as the law provides.

When you file the complaint, you choose how the amount is settled:

- a refund — to your card, or money back on a cash booking — which is the default; or
- credit in your account, under the rules for credit in the terms (section 9): credit is never paid out and expires 12 months after the last movement on your balance.

A guest's refund goes to the card. The company decides the amount; how it is settled is your choice. A refund is sent when the complaint is decided; when the money reaches your account depends on your bank.

## 7. If you disagree

If the company rejects your complaint, it tells you why. You can reply with more information through the complaint.

If we cannot resolve the dispute, you can turn to the out-of-court consumer dispute resolution body of the country in which the cleaning was performed — in the Czech Republic the Czech Trade Inspection Authority (www.coi.cz) — or to a court.
