## 2. What we process and why

### Your account

Your name, e-mail address and phone number, your password (stored only in a form from which it cannot be read back), your language and market, your saved addresses, your notification settings, and your credit, loyalty points and Cleansia Plus membership. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and e-mail address from them. We need this data to provide your account and the service under the terms (performance of a contract).

### Your bookings

The address of the home, the floor and flat number, how the cleaner gets in and your instructions for it, your notes, the services, the number of rooms and bathrooms, the level of dirtiness, the time, the price, how you pay, the state of the booking, a cancellation and its reason, and the rating you give. We need this data to perform the cleaning (performance of a contract). We also use your account, e-mail address and phone number to recognise your first booking, which has a longer window for free cancellation.

If you book as a guest, we process your name, e-mail address, phone number and the booking details above. The link in the booking e-mail works until 30 days after the cleaning.

### Payments and receipts

Card payments and saved cards are processed by Stripe; we never receive the full card number. For a saved card we keep its brand, last four digits and expiry, so that you can recognise it, and we keep Stripe's references to your payments. For a cash payment we record the amount the cleaner received and when. We also record the amounts you owe and their payment, your credit and its movements, and the promo codes you use. We need this data to take and settle payments (performance of a contract) and to keep accounts (legal obligation).

Receipts are issued in the company's name. A receipt names you and the address of the cleaning, never your e-mail address or phone number. We keep receipts because tax and accounting law requires it (legal obligation).

### Photos of your home

The cleaner photographs the home before and after the cleaning with the app's camera, as a record of the work done; if the cleaner cannot get in, they photograph the entrance. You, the cleaners on the job and our staff can see these photos. We take them in our legitimate interest in showing how the work was done and in settling complaints. How long we keep them is in section 4.

### What the cleaner sees

A cleaner deciding whether to take your booking sees it without your name, contact details, address and entry instructions. The cleaners who take it see your name, phone number, address and entry instructions from then until 24 hours after the cleaning is completed, and not at all once the booking is cancelled. Our staff see your entry instructions only when they deliberately reveal them, and every reveal is recorded.

### Complaints

When you file a complaint, we process its description, the messages between you and our staff, the photos you attach and how the complaint was settled. We need this data to decide the complaint (performance of a contract) and in our legitimate interest in settling and defending claims.

### The record of what you do

So that we can show what was agreed and what happened — for example when a fee or a refund is disputed — we record certain acts: creating an account, signing in and out, confirming your e-mail address, resetting your password, booking, cancelling, confirming a recurring visit, filing a complaint, changing your notification settings, subscribing to or cancelling Cleansia Plus, setting up and changing recurring cleanings, accepting the terms and this policy, and downloading your data. Each record holds the time, the IP address, the device and the facts of the act, such as the price and the cancellation terms shown to you; a refused attempt is recorded too. A record never holds your name, contact details, address, entry instructions, a text you wrote or card data. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in proving and defending claims.

When you book, we also record which versions of the terms and of this policy the booking is made under, and your express request to start the cleaning within the withdrawal period. Your acceptance of the terms and of this policy is recorded with its version, time, IP address and device.

### Notifications and devices

To send push notifications to your phone, we keep its push token and an identifier of the device. In-app notifications are kept in your notification list.

### Location

The customer app can, if you allow it, use your phone's location to centre the map and to find your address when you enter one; to find the address, the app sends the location to Mapbox. We keep the address you confirm and its map coordinates, not your phone's location.

In the partner app for cleaners, the phone's location is used only while the app is open: to show on the phone how far each job is — calculated on the phone and never sent to us — and to help enter an address. A cleaner's registered home address is converted into map coordinates so that we can send notifications about new jobs within the radius the cleaner chooses; without a chosen radius, no such limit applies. The home address never decides which jobs a cleaner may see or take. We do not track anyone's location.

### Marketing

Our only marketing is promotional push notifications. We send them only if you turn them on in your notification settings; they are off until you do, and you can turn them off there at any time. The legal basis is your consent. We send no marketing e-mails or text messages. E-mails about your bookings, payments and account are part of the service, not marketing.

### Cookies and storage on your device

The website uses only what it needs to work: the sign-in cookies, which scripts on the page cannot read; cookies that remember your language and market for a year; and, in your browser's local storage, the companions of your sign-in (a token protecting you against forged requests, your role and when the sign-in expires), your language, your colour theme and the fact that you have seen the cookie notice. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and the fonts are served from our own servers. When you pay by card on the website, the payment page is provided by Stripe, which uses its own cookies to keep payments secure.

### If you work with us as a cleaner

If you use the partner app as a cleaner, we also process your identity and contact details, your home address, nationality and identity document number, your emergency contact, the documents you upload, your payout bank details, the jobs you take, your pay, your invoices and the cash you hold for the company, the ratings customers give, and your acceptances of your contract documents with the IP address and the device. We need this data for the contract with you and to meet our legal obligations, such as keeping accounts.

## 3. Who receives your data

- The cleaners who take your booking, to the extent described in section 2.
- The companies that process data for us on our instructions, listed below.
- Google or Apple, if you choose to sign in with them.
- Public authorities, such as the tax authority or a court, where the law requires it.

The companies that process data for us:

- Stripe — card payments and saved cards.
- Microsoft Azure — hosting, the database and file storage, in data centres in the European Union.
- SendGrid — sending e-mails.
- Mapbox — address search, maps and converting addresses and locations into each other.
- Sentry — reports of errors and crashes in our apps and servers.
- Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service — delivering push notifications to your phone.

We do not sell your personal data.

Some of these companies are based outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Where your data is transferred there, the transfer relies on a decision of the European Commission that the country ensures adequate protection, or on the standard contractual clauses approved by the Commission. You can ask us for details.

## 4. How long we keep it

- Your account: while you have it; when you delete it, section 5 applies.
- The personal details on a booking — your name, contact details, address, floor and flat, how the cleaner gets in, your instructions and notes, a cancellation reason you gave and your rating: 2 years from the date of the cleaning, for a completed or cancelled booking; then they are anonymised.
- Photos of your home: 7 days after the cleaning is completed or the booking is cancelled; while a complaint about the booking is open, until it is decided. A booking that is neither completed nor cancelled keeps its photos until it is.
- Receipts: 10 years from the end of the year in which they were issued; after that the document is deleted and only the record of its number and the sale remains.
- The record of what you do: 3 years from each act.
- Your acceptance of the terms and of this policy: while you have your account, and 3 years after it is deleted.
- Complaints: while you have your account; when it is deleted, the attached photos are deleted at once and the text 3 years later.
- In-app notifications: 90 days.
- A phone registered for push notifications: 90 days after it was last used or signed out.
- One-time codes for confirming your e-mail address or resetting your password: until they expire.
- A request to exercise your rights: kept as a record of how it was handled; the name of the staff member who handled it is removed after 3 years.
- For cleaners: a document that has been replaced, 365 days after it was replaced; the IP address and device on the acceptance of a contract document, 3 years from the acceptance; the record of what a cleaner does in the app, 3 years from each act. Pay records and invoices are accounting records and are kept.

## 5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself in the app or on the website. It cannot be deleted while you have a booking that is not finished.

When your account is deleted, we anonymise it and your bookings, delete your saved addresses, the photos of your home and the files attached to your complaints, sign you out on every device and remove the IP address and device from the record of what you did. Finished guest bookings made with your e-mail address are anonymised with it. Unused credit is forfeited (terms, section 9). We keep only what the law or the defence of claims requires, for the periods in section 4: receipts, the record of what you did without the IP address and device, and the text of your complaints.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to:

- access your data and receive a copy of it;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have your data erased where there is no longer a reason to keep it;
- have the processing restricted while a question about it is being resolved;
- receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format and pass it to someone else;
- object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
- withdraw your consent to promotional notifications at any time, without affecting what was done before;
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in the Czech Republic the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, www.uoou.cz), or the authority of the European Union country where you live or work.

In the app and on the website you can see and correct your details, download a copy of your data, turn promotional notifications on or off and delete your account. For anything else, write to us at privacy@cleansia.cz.

We answer within one month. For a complex request we may extend this by two further months; we tell you so, with the reason, within the first month.

## 7. Age

Our services are for people aged 18 or over.

## 8. Automated decisions

We make no decision about you based solely on automated processing that has legal effects on you or similarly significantly affects you. Some rules are applied automatically — for example whether cash payment is available for a booking or which window for free cancellation applies; they are set out in the terms. If you think one was applied to you wrongly, write to us and a person will review it.

## 9. Security

Connections to our apps and website are encrypted, passwords are stored only in a form from which they cannot be read back, a link to a photo of your home works for 15 minutes, and our staff see only what their role needs.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We publish each version of this policy with the date it takes effect. When a new version takes effect, you are asked to accept it before your next booking.
