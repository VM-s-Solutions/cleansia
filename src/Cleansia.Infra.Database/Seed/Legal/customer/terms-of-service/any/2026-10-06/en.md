The price follows from the services, packages and extras you choose, the number of rooms and bathrooms, the level of dirtiness and the start time, and it is shown to you before you book. The price shown when you book is final; where the company is registered for VAT, it includes VAT.

## 5. The level of dirtiness

When you book, you choose how dirty the home is: normal, increased (+15 %) or heavy (+30 %). The surcharge applies to the price of all the services, packages and extras you choose, and the level also lengthens the booked time. Each level is described when you choose it; if you are unsure, choose the higher one. The level cannot be changed after booking.

A higher level or a larger job can need more than one cleaner, and a booking that needs more than one cleaner is paid by card.

## 6. Booking

You book by choosing the services, the level of dirtiness, the address and the start time, checking the summary and the price, and confirming. The contract is concluded when you confirm the booking — with a card payment, once the payment is completed; a booking whose card payment is not completed is cancelled. We confirm the booking by e-mail.

We then offer the job to the cleaners. If no cleaner has accepted it by its start time, we cancel it as described in section 14.

A recurring schedule is a Cleansia Plus benefit. Each visit is created as a booking that you confirm; a visit you do not confirm in time is cancelled without a fee.

## 7. Payment

You pay by card, through our payment provider Stripe, when you book.

You can pay in cash only when you are signed in and the booking needs one cleaner. You pay the price in cash to the cleaner after the cleaning. The cleaner collects it on behalf of the company, so paying the cleaner settles what you owe the company. Credit cannot be used on a cash booking.

For a cash booking you also need no unpaid amount owed to any operating company (section 8) and no more than two unpaid cash bookings at a time, counting this one.

We issue a receipt in the company's name once the payment has been received: for a card payment when it is completed, and for cash once the cleaner has recorded the payment and the cleaning is completed. We send the receipt by e-mail.

While an amount is owed, you cannot book cash; you can still book and pay by card.

## 9. Credit

Credit is a balance in your account that the company grants, for example as an apology when a cleaner does not arrive, as a goodwill gesture, as the settlement of a complaint for which you chose credit, or as a referral reward.

A friend can enter your referral code when creating their account, or later on a booking made with their account. A customer can be referred only once, and cannot use their own code. Two accounts of people who live in one home — a family, a couple or flatmates — count as one customer, so a referral between them is refused. If, within 90 days of the code being accepted, one of their bookings is completed and none of their bookings has been completed before it, each of you receives referral credit in the amount set for the currency it is paid in: your friend in the currency of that booking, and you in the currency of the last booking created on your account before the credit is paid, a cancelled one and an unconfirmed Cleansia Plus visit included, or, if your account has none, in the currency of your friend's booking. Where no amount is set for a currency, whichever of you would be paid in it receives no referral credit. The app shows the figure for the market chosen in it. The rules of this section apply to that credit.

The company may take back the referral credit from both of you if the booking that earned it is refunded, or if the referral was not genuine — for example, when a customer refers themselves through another account, refers someone they live with, or otherwise breaks the rules of this section. It never takes back more than the referral credit it gave you, nor more than your balance in that credit's currency holds at the time, so your balance never goes below zero.

If your account and your friend's appear to belong to the same person or to people who live in one home — for example, because they share an address, a phone number or an e-mail inbox — the company may hold the referral credit until a member of its staff has reviewed the referral, which it does without undue delay. It pays the referral credit once it finds the referral genuine; otherwise it refuses the referral and neither of you receives referral credit.

If a booking you paid partly with credit is cancelled or refunded, the amount returned is split between your balance and your card in the same proportion as you paid the price: all the credit you spent returns when nothing is kept, and only a proportional part of it when a fee is kept or only part of the price is refunded.

- Credit is never paid out, in cash or to a bank account.

- Unused credit is forfeited when your account is deleted.
- If the company stops providing the service, you can spend your credit until its last day of service; unused credit is written off once the company closes.

## 10. Cleansia Plus

Cleansia Plus is a paid membership, monthly or annual, sold at the price shown for your market; where no price is shown, it is not offered. The first membership on your account starts with a free trial of 14 days, on either plan; each account gets one free trial only. Your card is first charged when the trial ends, unless you cancel before then. The membership then renews automatically at the end of each period and is charged to your card until you cancel it. It keeps the currency in which you bought it.

The benefits apply from the first day, free trial included, for as long as the membership is current: a discount on every cleaning; free cancellation until 4 hours before the start instead of 24 hours; free cancellation within 60 minutes of booking; a number of express surcharges waived in each calendar month; recurring schedules; and choosing a preferred cleaner. The discount and the number of waivers are stated for your plan when you subscribe.

If a payment fails, whether the first one after the trial or a renewal, the benefits pause until the payment goes through. You can cancel at any time. If you cancel during the free trial, the membership ends when the trial ends and you are not charged; otherwise it ends at the end of the period you paid for, or immediately if a payment failed.

## 11. Loyalty points

A completed booking earns loyalty points in proportion to its price, at the rate shown in the app for its currency. A refund removes points in the same proportion, and a cancelled booking earns none. Your tier follows your points: it rises as you collect points and falls when points are removed. A tier may give a discount on bookings above the minimum amount shown in the app. Points have no cash value.

## 12. Promo codes and combined discounts

Promo codes are for signed-in customers. You can use one code per booking, within the validity, the number of uses, the currency and the minimum order amount stated for it.

The Cleansia Plus discount and the tier discount add up to at most 12 % of the price before discounts and the express surcharge. A promo code is not added to them: if the code gives more, it replaces them; otherwise they apply and the code does not. If a code no longer applies when you confirm, the booking is not made, rather than being charged at the full price.

## 13. Cancellation

You cancel a booking in the app or on the website; as a guest, through the link in the booking e-mail. The cancellation fee is a share of the price:

- Free while no cleaner has accepted the booking.
- Free within 60 minutes of booking on your first booking or while you have the Cleansia Plus benefits (section 10), and within 15 minutes of booking otherwise — even if a cleaner has already accepted it. Your first booking is recognised by your account, e-mail or phone, guest bookings included.
- Otherwise, once a cleaner has accepted: free until 24 hours before the start, 25 % between 24 and 4 hours before the start, and 50 % less than 4 hours before the start. While you have the Cleansia Plus benefits: free until 4 hours before the start and 50 % after that.
- Once the start time has passed with a cleaner assigned, you cannot cancel; if the cleaner has not arrived, report it (section 14). A cleaning in progress cannot be cancelled.

On a card booking the company keeps the fee from what you paid by card and in credit, in the same proportion as you paid the price, and returns the rest the same way: the card share to your card and the credit share to your balance. On a cash booking the fee is owed and is paid as described in section 8.

There is no rescheduling. To change the date or time, cancel the booking under these rules and book again.

We send a card refund without undue delay, at the latest within 3 days, and credit returns to your balance at once; when the money appears on your account depends on your bank.

## 14. When no cleaner arrives

If no cleaner has accepted your booking by its start time, we cancel it. If the assigned cleaner has not arrived after the start time, report it in the app or on the website, or as a guest through the link in the booking e-mail; our staff verify it and cancel the booking.

In both cases you pay nothing: we send the refund of a card payment in full at the latest within 3 days, and credit you spent returns to your balance at once; when the money appears on your account depends on your bank. With an account you also receive an apology credit in the amount shown in the app for your currency. The credit is a goodwill gesture on top of the refund; it does not replace any claim you have, and you waive nothing by receiving it. If one of several cleaners does not arrive, we settle it as a complaint (section 17).

## 15. When the cleaner cannot get into your home

Make sure the cleaner can get into the home at the booked time. If they cannot, the cleaner waits 15 minutes past the start, tries to reach you, and reports it with a photo of the entrance. When our staff confirm the report, the booking is cancelled as your cancellation at 100 % of the price: the company keeps a card payment and the credit applied to it, and on a cash booking the whole price is owed and paid as described in section 8. From a guest, the company keeps the payment and charges nothing more.

A tip you give the cleaner in person is a matter between you and the cleaner. The platform does not collect or record tips, and they are not part of the price.
