> Draft — this wording has not yet been through legal review and is not the final, binding version.

This agreement sets how you process the personal data of the company's customers when you perform jobs — as the processor, for the company as the controller — under Article 28 of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679. It completes the Framework Cooperation Agreement.

## 1. The parties

The controller: {{companyLegalName}}, company ID (IČO) {{companyRegistrationNumber}}, registered office {{companySeat}}, e-mail {{companyEmail}}, phone {{companyPhone}} (the "company").

The processor: you, the contractor under the Framework Cooperation Agreement.

## 2. What you process and why

You process customers' personal data only to perform the jobs you take, and only through the Cleansia partner app and partner website:

- the customer's name and phone number;
- the address of the home, the floor and flat, how to get in and the customer's instructions for it, such as a door code or where a key is;
- the customer's notes and the details of the home and the booking, such as pets and the services booked;
- the photos of the home you take before and after the cleaning, and the photo of the entrance when you cannot get in.

The people concerned are the company's customers and the people who live in or are present at the home. You do not process special categories of data, such as health data; if a customer tells you something of that kind, use it only to do the job safely and do not record it.

## 3. What the app gives you, and for how long

While you are deciding whether to take a job, the app shows it without the customer's name, contact details, address and entry instructions. Once you take the job, it shows them until 24 hours after the cleaning is completed, and not at all once the booking is cancelled. The first time you open a job's entry instructions is recorded.

A link to a photo of the home works for 15 minutes. You can delete a photo you took until the job is completed or cancelled. The company keeps the photos for 7 days after the cleaning is completed or the booking cancelled, and longer while a complaint about the booking is open.

## 4. Your obligations

- Use the data only for the job, and only as this agreement and the app allow. Never use it to contact the customer about anything else or to offer them anything.
- Do not copy the data: no screenshots, no notes, no photos or recordings outside the app, no forwarding. Take job photos only with the app's camera, never with your phone's own camera or from its gallery. If you use the partner website and your browser keeps a copy of a photo, delete it once the photo is uploaded.
- Do not take any other photos or recordings in the customer's home.
- Do not let anyone else see the data or use your account, and do not pass the data to anyone. You may not engage another processor.
- Protect your phone with a PIN, a password or biometrics, and keep your sign-in details to yourself.
- Keep the data confidential, also after this agreement ends.

## 5. Helping the company

If a customer asks you about their personal data, refer them to the company at {{companyEmail}}.

Tell the company without undue delay, and at the latest within 24 hours, if customers' data may have reached someone who should not have it — for example if your phone is lost or stolen, someone else saw the data or you sent a photo to the wrong person. Say what happened, when, which data and which customers it concerns and what you have done about it. If your phone is lost or stolen, also change your password.

Give the company the information it needs to show that this agreement is kept, and help it deal with a customer's request, a data breach or a question from the supervisory authority.

## 6. When the processing ends

Your access to a job's customer data ends as section 3 says. If, contrary to section 4, you have any customer data outside the app, delete it at the latest when your access to it ends. When this agreement ends, delete any customer data you still have and, if the company asks, confirm in writing that you have done so.

## 7. The company's obligations

The company gives you only the data you need for the job, processes it on a valid legal basis and informs its customers about the processing in its privacy policy. It tells you of changes that affect your obligations under this agreement.

## 8. Liability and contractual penalty

You are liable for damage caused by a breach of this agreement, as the General Data Protection Regulation and the law provide.

If, intentionally or through gross negligence and contrary to section 4, you keep, copy, pass on or use customers' data or photos of a home, you pay the company a contractual penalty equal to ten times your reward for the job whose data the breach concerned, once for each breach. The company may claim damages for the same breach only to the extent they exceed the penalty, and the same breach is not penalised under the Framework Cooperation Agreement.

## 9. Final provisions

This agreement lasts as long as the Framework Cooperation Agreement. In matters of personal data it takes precedence over the Framework Cooperation Agreement. It is governed by the same law.

The company publishes each new version of this agreement in the app with the date it takes effect. Until you accept the version in force, you cannot be approved or take new jobs.

This agreement is concluded when you accept it in the app. The company records which version you accepted, when, and from which device and IP address.
