title: Framework Cooperation Agreement
---

> Draft — this wording has not yet been through legal review and is not the final, binding version.

This agreement sets the terms on which you, as an independent contractor, clean homes for the operating company through the Cleansia partner app and partner website. Each job you take is a separate contract for work concluded under this agreement. Together with this agreement you accept the Self-Billing Agreement and the Data Processing Agreement.

## 1. The parties

The company is the operating company of the market in which you work — until your account is approved, of the market in which your address is:

{{companyLegalName}}, company ID (IČO) {{companyRegistrationNumber}}, registered office {{companySeat}}, e-mail {{companyEmail}}, phone {{companyPhone}} (the "company").

The contractor is you — the self-employed person who accepts this agreement in the partner app or on the partner website, identified by the details in your profile: your name, company ID (IČO), address and payout account (the "contractor" or "you").

## 2. What the company buys from you

The company sells cleaning to its customers in its own name and is responsible to them for it. It buys the work needed for that cleaning, as a subcontract, from independent contractors like you. You work for the company, not for the customer: you are not a party to the contract with the customer, and you sell the customer nothing.

The company pays you a reward for each job you perform (section 9). No commission is charged: you pay the company nothing for using the app, and the price the customer pays belongs to the company.

Each job is a separate contract for work between the company as the client and you as the contractor. It is formed as the contract for work shown with the job says, and it states your reward for that job.

## 3. Your independence

You run your business under your own trade licence, in your own name, on your own account and at your own risk. This agreement does not create an employment relationship. You pay your own income tax and your own social security and health insurance contributions.

You decide whether, when and how much you work. You are not obliged to take any job, to be available at any particular time or to work any minimum amount. You may work for anyone else, including other platforms and your own customers; the only limit concerns customers you meet through the platform (section 13).

You choose how to do the work. The company checks the result — whether the booked scope was cleaned, from the photos and its customers' complaints — not how you did it.

You must be at least 18 years old. You perform each job personally: you may not pass a job, or a customer's details, to anyone else. If you cannot do a job, release it (section 6).

## 4. Equipment and products

You bring your own cleaning products and equipment to every job; they are included in your reward and their cost is yours. They must be in good condition, safe and suitable for household surfaces.

## 5. Approval and your documents

You can take jobs once the company has approved your account. A person decides on the approval after checking that:

- your profile is complete, including your payout account;
- every document the country you will work in requires has been uploaded and accepted — among them an identity document, a valid certificate of the liability insurance under section 11, and a work permit if you need one;
- you have accepted this agreement, the Self-Billing Agreement and the Data Processing Agreement in the versions in force; and
- the company operates in that country.

Keep your profile, your documents and your payout account up to date, and replace a document before it expires.

## 6. How jobs are offered

The page "How jobs are offered" in the partner app and on the partner website describes these rules in more detail. The company applies them to every contractor alike:

- Every approved contractor sees the same open jobs: the jobs in the market you work in, in the currency you are paid in. A free place on a job goes to whoever takes it first.
- A customer you have already cleaned for can ask for you. You then have the first place on that job to yourself for a limited time; if you do not take it, or you release it, it goes to everyone.
- The company notifies you of new jobs. If you choose a distance from your address, only of jobs within it; the board still lists every job.
- A few steps happen automatically: a customer's request for you ends at its deadline; a job with nobody on it shortly after its start is cancelled and the customer refunded; you are reminded of your jobs; and the company's administrators are told when a job you took has not been started shortly after its start. None of these steps decides anything about you.
- No score decides your access to jobs. The company does not rank contractors, and ratings do not change which jobs you see or can take. An administrator may set you a weekly limit on jobs, with a reason you see in your profile.
- While you hold more of the company's cash than the limit the company sets, jobs paid in cash are hidden from you (section 8). The app shows you the limit and the reason.

Before you take a job, the app shows you the job and its contract for work with your reward for your place on it. Taking the job forms the contract for work.

An administrator may also place you on a job. A placement is an offer: you may decline it, without any consequence, by releasing the job. If you keep it, you accept the contract for work in the app before you start.

You may release a job you have taken — ask for cover or drop it — at any time before it is finished. Releasing costs you nothing and does not change which jobs you are offered, but do it as early as you can: the customer is counting on the cleaning. The company takes you off a job only with a written reason, which you can read in the app.

## 7. Doing the job

Arrive at the agreed time and clean the scope that was booked. Extra work cannot be ordered on site: the customer has to book it. Record in the app when you set off, when you start and when you finish.

Take the job photos with the app's camera: before photos from when you hold the job until it is finished, and after photos while the work is under way. You cannot complete a job without an after photo.

If you cannot get into the home, wait 15 minutes past the start, try to reach the customer, photograph the entrance with the app and report it in the app with a note of the calls you made. The company decides what happens next.

If something is damaged, or you find damage that was there before you started, photograph it and tell the company without delay.

The platform does not collect or record tips. A tip a customer gives you in person is a matter between you and the customer and is not part of your reward.

## 8. Cash you collect for the company

On a booking paid in cash, the customer pays the price to you in cash after the cleaning. You collect it for the company: the money belongs to the company, and by paying you the customer settles what they owe the company. Record the payment in the app when you receive it; the app states the amount due, and you confirm it. If the customer pays less, tell the company. Accept cash only where the app lets you record it.

You hold the cash for the company until it is set off against your reward (section 10) or you hand it over. If you hold it after a monthly settlement for longer than the company sets, the company asks you to hand it over, and you do so as it instructs. While you hold more than the limit the company sets, jobs paid in cash are hidden from you; a job paid in cash that you already have stays yours.

## 9. Your reward

For each job you complete, you are paid the reward for your place on it. It follows from the company's rates for the services and packages booked, the number of rooms and bathrooms and the level of dirtiness, and it is divided equally among the places the job needs. It is stated in {{currency}}, the currency of the market you work in.

You see the reward before you take the job, and it is stated in the job's contract for work. The reward on your pay record is calculated by the same rules when the job is completed; if it differs from the reward in the contract for work by more than rounding, tell the company and it will correct it.

A job you do not perform — released, taken off you or cancelled — earns no reward, with one exception: when a customer cancels late or does not let you in and the company collects a fee for it, you are paid half of the fee collected, divided among the places on the job. Nothing is paid on a fee that is owed but not collected.

The company may add a bonus. It deducts from your reward only:

- a charge for a customer's complaint about your job, when a person at the company concludes, after reviewing the complaint, that you were at fault; the charge is linked to the complaint, never exceeds your reward for that job, and you can read its reason in the app;
- the cash you hold for the company (section 10); and
- an amount you owe the company under this agreement that you have acknowledged or that a court has decided.

A refund to a customer on its own never reduces your reward.

## 10. Monthly settlement

The company settles your rewards monthly. After each monthly pay period closes, it issues in your name, under the Self-Billing Agreement, an invoice for your rewards in that period — one for each currency — and sends it to you.

The cash you hold for the company in that currency is set off against the invoice, up to its total. The company transfers the rest to the payout account in your profile by the due date on the invoice; cash the invoice did not cover stays recorded with you until the next invoice or until you hand it over.

## 11. Liability and insurance

You are liable for damage you cause, while performing a job, to the customer's property, to the company or to anyone else, including the loss of keys or entry codes entrusted to you. The company deals with its customer's complaint; if it compensates its customer for damage you caused, it may claim that amount from you to the extent your insurance does not pay it.

For as long as this agreement lasts, you keep business liability insurance with an adequate limit, covering damage to property and health caused while cleaning, including damage to things entrusted to you. You give the company a valid certificate of the insurance before approval and whenever it asks, and you tell it without delay if the insurance ends or its cover is reduced.

## 12. Customer data and confidentiality

You process the personal data of the company's customers only as the Data Processing Agreement says. Keep confidential everything you learn about customers and their homes and about the company's business, both while this agreement lasts and after it ends.

## 13. Customers you meet through the platform

While this agreement lasts and for 12 months after your last job for a customer, you will not offer or provide cleaning or other household services to a customer you met through the platform other than through the platform, whether yourself or through someone else, and you will not give such a customer your contact details for that purpose. If a customer asks you to work for them directly, ask them to book the cleaning in the app.

This does not apply to a customer you knew before you met them through the platform. There is no other restriction on competition: you may work for anyone else, including other platforms.

## 14. Contractual penalties

If you breach section 13 with a customer, you pay the company a contractual penalty equal to ten times your reward for your last job for that customer, once for each customer concerned. The only penalty for misuse of customer data is the one in the Data Processing Agreement.

The company may claim damages for the same breach only to the extent they exceed the penalty. A breach for which one of these agreements sets a penalty is not penalised under the other.

## 15. Your data and decisions about you

The company processes your personal data as its privacy policy describes. It does not track your location. No automated system decides on your approval, your access to jobs, taking you off a job or the end of this agreement: a person does. If you disagree with a decision about your account or your jobs, write to the company at {{companyEmail}}; a person will review it and answer you.

## 16. Duration and ending

This agreement is concluded for an indefinite period. You may end it at any time by telling the company. The company may end it by notice, with a notice period of one month that starts on the first day of the month after the notice is delivered to you.

The company may end this agreement with immediate effect for a serious breach: for example theft, fraud or violence, a serious breach of the Data Processing Agreement or of section 13, the loss of your trade licence or of your insurance, or repeatedly not coming to jobs you had taken and not released. A person decides, and you can ask for the reason.

Once this agreement has ended, you cannot take new jobs. Jobs you already have are finished or released. Your last rewards are invoiced and paid, with the cash you hold set off, at the next monthly settlement, and you hand over any cash you still hold for the company.

## 17. Changes

The company publishes each new version of this agreement in the app with the date it takes effect. Until you accept the version in force, you cannot be approved or take new jobs; a job you already have continues under its contract for work. If you do not agree with a new version, you may end this agreement.

## 18. Final provisions

This agreement is governed by the law of the country in which the company has its registered office, and its courts decide disputes the parties do not settle by agreement.

The company communicates with you through the app and by e-mail to the address in your profile; you write to the company at {{companyEmail}}.

This agreement is concluded when you accept it in the app. The company records which version you accepted, when, and from which device and IP address.
