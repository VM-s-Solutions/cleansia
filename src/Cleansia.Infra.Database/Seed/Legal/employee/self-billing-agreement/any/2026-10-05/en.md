title: Self-Billing Agreement
---

> Draft — this wording has not yet been through legal review and is not the final, binding version.

With this agreement you authorise the operating company to issue the invoices for your work in your name, so that you do not have to issue them yourself. It completes the Framework Cooperation Agreement and uses its terms.

## 1. The parties

The company: {{companyLegalName}}, company ID (IČO) {{companyRegistrationNumber}}, registered office {{companySeat}}, e-mail {{companyEmail}}, phone {{companyPhone}} (the "company"), as the customer.

You: the contractor under the Framework Cooperation Agreement, identified by the details in your profile, as the supplier.

## 2. What you authorise

You authorise the company to issue, in your name and on your behalf, the invoices for the rewards it owes you under the Framework Cooperation Agreement and the contracts for work — for the jobs you complete or cannot perform because the customer does not let you in, and for your share of a collected late-cancellation fee. You do not issue your own invoices for the same rewards.

## 3. When and how the company invoices

After each pay period of 14 days closes, the company issues, for your rewards in that period, one invoice for each currency in which you earned them. It numbers the invoice and gives it a payment reference in its own series for these invoices.

The invoice names you as the supplier — with your name, company ID, address and payout account as they are in your profile — and the company as the customer. It states the period, the jobs and the late-cancellation fee shares it covers, any bonus and deduction, the total, the cash you hold for the company that is set off against it, the amount to be transferred to you and the due date.

The company sends you the invoice by e-mail, and you can also find it in the app. It pays the amount to be transferred by the due date, as the Framework Cooperation Agreement says.

## 4. Checking an invoice

Check every invoice. If something in it is wrong, tell the company within 14 days of receiving it. If you do not object within that time, you accept the invoice as issued.

Send your objection to {{companyEmail}}. The company reviews it and, where it is justified, corrects or cancels the invoice and issues a corrected one.

## 5. VAT and your taxes

You declare that you are not a VAT payer. The invoices are issued for a supplier who is not a VAT payer and carry no VAT.

If you become a VAT payer, tell the company before the date from which you are registered. The company cannot issue invoices with VAT in your name through the app, so from that date it stops issuing your invoices under this agreement, and the parties agree how your rewards will be invoiced.

The invoices issued in your name are the records of your income. Keep them, and declare and pay the tax on your income yourself.

## 6. Your details

The company issues the invoices from the details in your profile. Keep your name, company ID, address and payout account correct and up to date; you are responsible for details you gave wrongly or did not update.

## 7. Duration and changes

This agreement lasts as long as the Framework Cooperation Agreement and ends with it, once the invoice for your last rewards has been issued.

The company publishes each new version of this agreement in the app with the date it takes effect. Until you accept the version in force, you cannot be approved or take new jobs.

This agreement is concluded when you accept it in the app. The company records which version you accepted, when, and from which device and IP address.
