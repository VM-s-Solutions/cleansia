This contract for work is concluded between the customer who booked the order (the client) and the cleaner who accepts it through the Cleansia application (the contractor). Cleansia operates the application and is not a party to this contract.

## Formation

The contract is formed the moment the cleaner accepts the order in the application. The order is offered on the wording of this contract in the version in force when the customer booked it, and a later version does not change a contract already formed.

## Subject of the work

The contractor undertakes to perform the cleaning job identified by the order number, date, time window, price and location shown to you at acceptance, to the scope shown there: the number of rooms and bathrooms, the selected services, packages and extras.

## Price

The price of the work is the amount shown to you at acceptance, stated in {{currency}}, and is the final amount payable by the client. The price is collected through the Cleansia platform under the platform's terms; the contractor is remunerated by the platform under a separate agreement.

## Performance

The contractor performs the work personally, with professional care, at the agreed time and place, using appropriate equipment and products. The client provides access to the premises at the agreed time and the conditions needed to carry out the work. The parties communicate about the job through the application.

## Liability and complaints

The contractor is liable for damage caused to the client's property while performing the work. A defect in the work or damage is reported by the client through the application's dispute system within the period stated in the customer terms of service; the parties cooperate in resolving it.

## Supporting terms

Matters not covered by this contract are governed by Cleansia's Terms of Service for customers in the version in force when the order was booked, and by the law of the market where the work is performed.
