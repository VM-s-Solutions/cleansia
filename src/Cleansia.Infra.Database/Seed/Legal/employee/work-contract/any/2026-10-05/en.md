This contract for work is concluded for one cleaning job between the operating company that sold the cleaning to its customer (the client) and you, the cleaner who accepts it in the Cleansia partner app or on the partner website (the contractor), under the Framework Cooperation Agreement between you.

## The client

{{companyLegalName}}, company ID (IČO) {{companyRegistrationNumber}}, registered office {{companySeat}}.

## Formation

The contract is formed when you take the job after reading this contract and the job shown with it. If the client placed you on the job, the placement is an offer you may decline, without any consequence, by releasing the job; the contract is then formed when you accept it in the app, which you do before you start.

The job is offered on this contract in the version in force when its customer booked it; a later version does not change a contract already formed.

## Subject of the work

You undertake to perform the cleaning job identified by the order number, date, time window and location shown to you at acceptance, to the scope shown there: the number of rooms and bathrooms and the services, packages and extras booked. The work is done in the home of the client's customer.

## Price

The price of the work is the reward for your place on the job shown to you at acceptance, stated in {{currency}}. It is not the price the customer pays the client.

The client pays the price as the Framework Cooperation Agreement says: on the invoice issued in your name under the Self-Billing Agreement, with the cash you hold for the client set off. Cash you collect from the customer on a booking paid in cash belongs to the client and is not the price of your work.

## Performance

You perform the work personally, with professional care, at the agreed time and place, with your own equipment and products, and only to the booked scope. You take the photos before and after the work in the app and record in the app when you start and when you finish. The client arranges with its customer that you can get into the home at the agreed time; if you cannot get in, you proceed as the Framework Cooperation Agreement says.

## Completion

The work is completed when you record it in the app as completed, together with the photos of the finished work.

## If the job does not go ahead

The contract ends, with no liability for either party, when you release the job in the app, when the client takes you off it with a reason or when the booking is cancelled. Work not performed earns no price, with two exceptions: if the customer does not let you into the home and the client confirms it, you are paid the price of the work in full; if the client collects a fee for the customer's late cancellation, you receive your share of the fee collected under the Framework Cooperation Agreement.

## Liability and complaints

You are liable to the client for defects in the work and for damage you cause while performing it. The client deals with its customer's complaint; a charge to you for a complaint is made only as the Framework Cooperation Agreement says, when the client concludes that you were at fault.

## Supporting terms

Matters not covered by this contract are governed by the Framework Cooperation Agreement, the Self-Billing Agreement and the Data Processing Agreement in the versions you have accepted, and by the law of the country in which the client has its registered office.
